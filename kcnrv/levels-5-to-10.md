## At total level 5: groups and businesses

`/creategroup` makes a permanent [group](../groups-and-parties/) for owning things together. A group can hold up to
three business slots.

`/businesses` leases a [business](../businesses/) slot for 7, 14, 21 or 30 real days:

| Business | Lease, a day | Earns, a real hour at full condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car washes (Strawberry, Little Seoul) | $2,500 | $250 |
| Breaking Bad Scrapyard | $3,000 | $300 |
| Tequilala | $3,500 | $350 |
| Bahama Mamas, Vanilla Unicorn | $4,000 | $400 |

The cheapest lease is $17,500: a car wash for 7 days.

- **The safe.** Earnings wait in a safe you collect in person. Leave it a day and it earns at 60%.
- **Jobs keep it running.** A run-down business earns a quarter of what it could. Business jobs pay the crew $3,000
  to $4,800, or $6,000 to $10,000 for a hit on a rival, and 40 Business XP.
- **Reputation.** Reputation 1 comes after 2 jobs. Reputation 3, after about 22, gives you a second slot.

## Buy a house

The goal pays $2,500 and 400 XP. You can spawn in your [house](../houses/), and <kbd>H</kbd> inside opens the owner
menu. The city adds 2% stamp duty to the price; property tax skips the first $250,000 of a home's value; selling back
to the city returns 80%.

Towing 6, about 21 kerb calls in, hands you the Flatbed and three new calls:

| Call | Base pay | XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Police seizure | $2,400 | 90 |
| Impound transfer | $1,800 | 70 |
| Gang car | $3,200 | 110 |

## Fishing 7

About 35 keepers in, you can buy the large cooler. It carries 140 lb (63.5 kg); the small one carries 60 lb (27 kg)
and your pockets 25 lb (11 kg). See [Fishing](../fishing/).

## Gear for banks

[Lester](../lester/) sells to civilians:

- a hacking device ($15,000) makes every hack one step easier, but burns out one time in four;
- a laptop ($100,000) is two steps and never breaks;
- a USB drive ($3,500), used in the laptop, covers one failed hack.
