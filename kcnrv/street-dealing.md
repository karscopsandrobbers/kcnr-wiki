Carry drugs or guns and you can work a spot: stand on a street corner and sell to the people who come to you, one at
a time. It is slower than selling to a gang's corner by the box, but it pays the full street price.

## Working a spot

There are spots all over the map: the gangs' corners, and dozens of street corners of their own. You find them on foot.
Walk past one with drugs or a gun in your pockets and a prompt shows how busy it is today: **Busy**, **Quiet** or
**Dry today**. Press <kbd>E</kbd> on **Work this corner**.

**Hot spots.** Three spots a day are on everybody's map, with a purple icon. They pay more. A new three every day at
midnight UTC.

While you work, bars at the bottom right show:

- **Demand**: how much the spot has left today. Each sale uses some up, and a dry spot fills back up over a few hours.
- **Turf**: whose ground you're on and what that gang thinks of you.
- **Price**: what you're asking, against the street price.
- **Takings**: what you've made here.

Stay close to the spot, on foot. Walk off, get in a vehicle, run out of things to sell, or let the spot run dry, and
you stop. <kbd>H</kbd> at the spot stops you too.

## Customers

Somebody turns up every half a minute to a minute, walking up from down the street. They stop in front of you and say
what they want and what they'll pay.

- <kbd>E</kbd> **Sell**: you hand it over and they hand you the cash, right there in the street for anyone to see.
- <kbd>G</kbd> **Send them away.**
- Leave them too long and they give up and go.

**Drugs.** A customer wants one to three doses of any drug: most often something you carry, sometimes something you
don't, and then they move on. They pay the drug's full street price by its purity, the same as a gang's dealer
charges for a bag.

**Guns.** Somebody carrying guns gets gun buyers: one gun each, at **a quarter of Ammu-Nation's price**. Carry both
and some customers want a gun. They're more common in the rough parts of town (Davis, Strawberry, Chamberlain Hills,
Rancho, Cypress Flats, El Burro Heights, La Mesa, Elysian Island, Sandy Shores, Grapeseed, Stab City) and where the local
gang is at war, and rarer in the city.

**Your price.** At the spot, <kbd>E</kbd> raises your price and <kbd>G</kbd> lowers it, from 20% under the street price
to 20% over. Cheaper brings customers sooner; dearer, and they're slower to come.

## The gangs' ground

Most rough streets are some gang's ground (see [Gangs and street crime](../gangs-and-street-crime/)).

- **A gang that counts you a friend** takes **a fifth** of every sale on its ground.
- **Anybody else** dealing there pays no cut, but the gang thinks a little less of you with every sale, and a gang
  that turns hostile comes for you the way it always does.

## The risks

- **An officer who sees a sale**: **Drug Trafficking**, or **Weapons Trafficking** for a gun, 2 stars.
- **Witnesses.** Somebody who watched the deal go down can phone it in, and you get the same 2 stars and the police are
  told. Only someone who actually saw it can. Wear a mask and they can't say who you are.
- **Undercover officers.** Now and then a customer is a cop. Sell to one and they take the goods, pull a gun on you,
  and you're wanted at once with the police on their way. There's no telling them apart.
- **Bad deals.** Now and then a customer pulls a knife or a pistol instead. <kbd>E</kbd> hands over half your cash,
  <kbd>G</kbd> half of each drug you carry (a gun if you have no drugs), or fight them off. Do nothing and they go for
  you. Robbers are more likely in the rough parts of town, at night, when you're carrying a lot of cash, and when
  you're selling cheap.

## Junkies

Junkies hang around the gangs' corners, slumped against a wall or smoking. Working a spot near them, one may come over
instead of a stranger. They want one dose of whatever you have and offer little for it: <kbd>Y</kbd> **Ask for more**,
and either they find a bit more or they walk off.

Now and then a junkie overdoses where they lie. Press <kbd>X</kbd> on them and **Call A Medic**: it goes to the medics on
duty as a real call. The first to take it gets the patient, and you get thanked (and a little XP, once in a while). If
no medic is on duty, or nobody comes in time, they die there.

## Corners

Every gang corner deals drugs. Those in the rough parts of town deal guns too: they buy boxes of product and crates of
guns and rounds, and sell their friends both. See [Dealing with a corner](../gangs-and-street-crime/).

## Your record

The stat cards on the <kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard count street customers served, guns sold on the street and what they
made, the times you were robbed, robbers you put down, sales to undercover officers, and medics called for a junkie.
Doses sold count with your [drug stats](../drugs/), and the money made selling drugs on the street has a leaderboard.
