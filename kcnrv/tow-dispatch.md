## When you can start

You must be a civilian at any level, not wanted, and not on another job. Any tow yard hands out a truck, free: the
**Tow Truck** at rank 1, the **Large Tow Truck** at rank 2, the **Flatbed** at rank 3. Only the driver hears the radio.

## How to start

1. Walk onto the marker at a tow yard: Rogers Salvage & Scrap in La Puerta, Red's Auto Parts in Strawberry, or Red's
   Auto Garage in Murrieta Heights, Sandy Shores and Paleto Bay.
2. Get behind the wheel. Tonya, the dispatcher, puts you on duty.
3. A call comes in every 25 to 50 seconds. The card shows the street, the car, the distance and the fee; press
   <kbd>Y</kbd> to take it, and the first driver to press it gets it. If the radio's quiet, <kbd>Y</kbd> asks for a call.
4. When you're done, hand the truck back from the menu at any yard.

## A call, start to finish

1. **Find the car.** It carries a cone until it's on your truck.
2. **Hook it.** A hook truck: reverse up to the car and press <kbd>Left Shift</kbd>. A flatbed: <kbd>X</kbd> lowers the
   ramp, then <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> winches the car up.
3. **Bring it in** to the right place: the LSPD Auto Impound for illegally parked cars, police seizures and gang cars;
   the nearest yard for abandoned, crashed, broken-down and insurance cars; another yard for an impound transfer; the
   crusher at Rogers for a chop job.
4. **Hand it over.** A hook truck: stop inside the ring with the car on. A flatbed: stop in the ring, then lower the
   ramp and unload.

**If things go wrong:** a truck on its roof gives you 45 seconds, and <kbd>Y</kbd> near it rights it for 10% of the fee
(at least $150). A car that came off the truck gives you 60 seconds to hook it again.

**A call fails** if time runs out, you die, your truck or the car is destroyed, the owner drives off with it, or you
type `/tow cancel`. It pays nothing, and there's a 20-second wait.

## What you find at the scene

- **Illegally parked:** the owner comes out shouting, then runs for the car. You only lose the job if they get 80 m
  away in it. Hook it first; three in ten of them then come at you.
- **Highway breakdown:** the driver rides in your cab and, alive at the yard, tips $200 to $800.
- **Police seizure:** an officer and a cruiser are on scene. Sometimes it's a real player's car seized by an officer.
- **Gang vehicle:** three or four of the crew stand nearby. If one of them sees it go (within 45 m, with a clear
  line), they all come. Shooting one of them counts as being seen.
- **Insurance recovery:** half the time a crew is stripping the car, and they turn on you when it goes on the hook.

## Pay

The fee is the call's base pay plus $0.24 a metre, out to the car and from the car to the drop-off. After dark (22:00
to 05:00 game time) the fee is 25% higher, and so is the XP.

| Call | Rank | Base pay | Towing XP | Civilian XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abandoned vehicle | 1 | $1,400 | 25 | 55 |
| Crashed vehicle | 1 | $1,700 | 35 | 65 |
| Illegally parked | 1 | $1,500 | 30 | 60 |
| Highway recovery | 2 | $2,000 + a $200 to $800 tip | 40 | 80 |
| Police seizure | 3 | $2,400 | 50 | 90 |
| Impound transfer | 3 | $1,800 | 45 | 70 |
| Gang vehicle | 3 | $3,200 | 55 | 110 |
| Insurance recovery | 4 | $4,000 | 70 | 130 |
| Chop order | 5 | The car's worth, $6,800 to $10,000, by its condition | 60 | 120 |

**For example:** an abandoned car 1 km out and 1 km back pays $1,880, or $2,350 at night.

## Getting better at it

Every delivery builds your **Towing** skill, and its level is your tow rank (**Skills & ranks** in <kbd>F10</kbd>):

| Rank | Towing level | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Rookie | 1 | Abandoned, crashed and illegally parked cars; the Tow Truck |
| 2 Recovery | 3 | Highway breakdowns and night calls; the Large Tow Truck |
| 3 Contracted | 6 | Police seizures, impound transfers and gang cars; the Flatbed; calls further out |
| 4 Insured | 10 | Insurance recoveries |
| 5 Connected | 15 | The scrapyard's offers and chop orders |

A rookie gets no calls at night.

**At rank 5, the scrapyard texts you an offer** when a car goes on your hook: two to four times the fee, depending on
the call. Press <kbd>Y</kbd> within 45 seconds and the car goes to the crusher instead. **It's a crime.** The gate
camera catches your plate half the time on a car the impound was expecting, and one time in five otherwise, for 2
wanted stars.

Delivering every kind of call at least once earns the **Tow Mater** achievement.

## Tips

- Stay in the driver's seat: getting out takes the card, and your <kbd>Y</kbd>, away.
- Night pays 25% more, but you need rank 2 before you get night calls.
- On a gang call, keep out of the crew's sight.
- Hook a parked car before the owner reaches it.
- Drive gently with a car on: righting the truck costs 10% of the fee every time.
- You can't hear the radio while you're wanted.
