## When you can start

You must be a civilian at any level, not wanted, and not on another job. Any tow yard hands out a truck, free: the
**Tow Truck** at rank 1, the **Large Tow Truck** at rank 2, the **Flatbed** at rank 3. The two newer tow truck models
also work the radio and hook cars, though the yard only hands out the three above.

You're on duty when you're the **driver of a tow truck from a yard**, as a civilian with nothing else on: only the
driver hears the radio, and a truck that didn't come from a yard isn't seen by dispatch. If you ask for a call and
can't have one, you're told why: you're not a civilian, you're on another job, in jail, being arrested or in the middle
of a robbery, you're wanted, or you're not in a tow truck.

## How to start

1. Walk onto the marker at a tow yard: Rogers Salvage & Scrap in La Puerta, Red's Auto Parts in Strawberry, or Red's
   Auto Garage in Murrieta Heights, Sandy Shores and Paleto Bay.
2. Get behind the wheel. Tonya, the dispatcher, puts you on duty.
3. Calls come in every so often, with at most two open at once. The card shows the street, the car, the
   distance and the fee; press <kbd>Y</kbd> within 25 seconds to take it, and the first driver to press it gets it. An
   untaken call lapses after a while. If the radio's quiet, <kbd>Y</kbd> asks for a call, but not too often, and not while a card is already up. You're not sent a second card while the first is showing.
4. When you're done, hand the truck back from the menu at any yard.

If every yard spot is taken when you sign on, the truck is put on a clear road nearby, with a waypoint and a blip.

**Between calls:** after a delivery dispatch gives you a **30-second** break; after a failed or cancelled call, 20
seconds.

### The show car at Del Perro Pier

Below level 10, Tonya sometimes phones you with a booked job (see [Your first hour](../your-first-hour/)): a show car
at the car meet on Del Perro Pier, going to storage once the judges are done with it, about 8 minutes after the call.
Press <kbd>Y</kbd> on the card after the call. If you're not in a tow truck, the nearest yard goes on your map; the
job is yours as soon as you're at the wheel of one, with no radio card. It pays as a yard-to-yard transfer, and the
clock includes the wait.

The owner won't let the car go until the judging is done: the hook and the winch won't take it, and your objective
says to wait. The first time Tonya books it, **get there early** and the owner lends you a Light Rod and 10 Sandworms
to fish off the end of the pier while you wait. They're yours to keep. When the judging is done the owner says so,
and it's an ordinary tow from there. Nobody turns up within 10 minutes of it being ready and the owner calls
somebody else.

## A call, start to finish

1. **Find the car.** It carries a cone until it's on your truck. Everyone at the scene has a blip: green for
   onlookers and a stranded driver, blue for an officer, yellow for the owner, red for a crew or a gang. The car is
   always one a hook can take: never a bike, boat, aircraft, train, trailer or tow truck.
2. **Hook it.** A hook truck: reverse up to the car and press <kbd>Left Shift</kbd>. A flatbed: <kbd>X</kbd> lowers the
   ramp, then <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> winches the car up. The winch takes the nearest car lined up behind the
   ramp, but the call's own car always wins. The winch and
   unload buttons only show once the ramp is down and has stopped moving, and you're told when nothing is in reach. A car on
   its roof is stood up before it's hooked. More on both below.
3. **Bring it in** to the right place: the LSPD Auto Impound for illegally parked cars, police seizures and gang cars;
   the nearest yard for abandoned, crashed, broken-down and insurance cars; another yard for an impound transfer; the
   crusher at Rogers for a chop job.
4. **Hand it over.** The drop-off ring shows from a distance. A hook truck: stop
   inside the ring with the car on. A flatbed: stop in the ring, lower the ramp and unload (the car coming off near
   the marker counts), then pull on past the marker to finish.

A delivered car is locked and empty, left where you put it (in a bay at the impound) and fades away 30 seconds later. A
real police seizure stays impounded for its owner.

**The clock:** you get time for the length of the run (out to the car and on to the drop-off), **plus** more for the
harder kinds of call: a crash gives you less than a gang vehicle or a chop order.
**If things go wrong:** a truck on its roof gives you 45 seconds, and <kbd>Y</kbd> next to it rights it
for 10% of the fee (at least $150). A car that came off the truck gives you 60 seconds to
hook it again, and a roof outranks that: righting the truck starts the 60 seconds. A red bar counts it down, and the cone
goes back over the car while it's off. Whatever way a call ends, its car is only taken away once nobody's sitting in it.

**A call fails** if time runs out, you die, your truck or the car is destroyed, the owner drives off with it, or you
type `/tow cancel`. It pays nothing, and there's a 20-second wait.

## Hooks, ramps and winches

From the driver's seat, a button bar at the bottom of the screen names what each key does right now:

| Truck | Key | The bar says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook truck | <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> | **Hook Vehicle** with a car right behind you, **Hook Vehicle (reverse up to it)** with none, **Unhook Vehicle** with one on the arm |
| Flatbed | <kbd>X</kbd> | **Lower Ramp**, **Raise Ramp**, or **Ramp Moving** while it travels |
| Flatbed | <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> | **Winch Vehicle** with a car in reach, **Winch Vehicle (back up to it)** with none, **Unload Vehicle** with one aboard |
| Either | <kbd>Y</kbd> | The tow radio |

- **The hook** needs the truck stopped ("Stop the truck first"). It lifts the car by the end facing the truck, puts a
  driver who isn't a player out on the kerb first, and takes a car upside down by standing it up. <kbd>Left Shift</kbd>
  again lets it go.
- **The winch** needs the car lined up straight behind the lowered ramp. If it can't get hold of
  the car or can't pull it on, you're told to try again or to line the ramp up straight. <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> with a car
  aboard unloads it back onto the road.
- **Neither takes** a boat, aircraft, train or another tow truck, a car with a player at the wheel, or anything big
  like a bus (your call's own car is always allowed). The hook won't lift a bicycle either.
- **They work off duty too**: any hook truck or flatbed you drive will carry a car, with no call and no pay.

## What you find at the scene

- **Abandoned:** filthy, with one flat tyre.
- **Crashed:** a wreck with the glass out, and one or two onlookers filming or watching.
- **Illegally parked:** the owner comes out shouting, then runs for the car. You only lose the job if
  they get away in it. Hook it first and they lose and are put out; some of them then come at you.
- **Highway breakdown:** the driver rides in your cab and, alive at the yard, tips $200 to $800.
- **Police seizure:** an officer and a cruiser are on scene. Sometimes it's a real player's car seized by an officer:
  see **A live seizure** below.
- **Impound transfer:** a car from the impound's history, waiting in a bay at the lot, to take to a yard away from it.
- **Gang vehicle:** a few of the crew stand nearby. If one of them sees it go, they all come. Shooting one of them counts as being seen.
- **Insurance recovery:** half the time a crew is stripping the car, and they turn on you when it goes on the hook.
- **Chop order:** sometimes the owner is there, and shouts before going for the car.

**A live seizure:** when an officer's arrest impounds a suspect's car and a rank 3 driver on duty is nearby, the car waits at the kerb for a couple of minutes as a call. The officer is told a tow has been called and who's on the
way. With no taker, the impound lot sends its own truck as before.

## Pay

The fee is the call's base pay plus $0.24 a metre, out to the car and from the car to the drop-off. After dark (22:00
to 05:00 game time) the fee is 25% higher, and so is the XP.

| Call | Rank | Base pay | Towing XP | Civilian XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abandoned vehicle | 1 | $1,400 | 25 | 55 |
| Crashed vehicle | 1 | $1,700 | 35 | 65 |
| Illegally parked | 1 | $1,500 | 30 | 60 |
| Highway recovery | 2 | $2,000 + a $200 to $800 tip | 40 | 80 |
| Police seizure | 3 | $2,400 | 50 | 90 |
| Impound transfer | 3 | $1,800 | 45 | 70 |
| Gang vehicle | 3 | $3,200 | 55 | 110 |
| Insurance recovery | 4 | $4,000 | 70 | 130 |
| Chop order | 5 | The car's worth, $6,800 to $10,000, by its condition | 60 | 120 |

**For example:** an abandoned car 1 km out and 1 km back pays $1,880, or $2,350 at night.

## Getting better at it

Every delivery builds your **Towing** skill, and its level is your tow rank (**Skills & ranks** in <kbd>F10</kbd>):

| Rank | Towing level | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Rookie | 1 | Abandoned, crashed and illegally parked cars; the Tow Truck |
| 2 Recovery | 3 | Highway breakdowns and night calls; the Large Tow Truck |
| 3 Contracted | 6 | Police seizures, impound transfers and gang cars; the Flatbed; calls further out |
| 4 Insured | 10 | Insurance recoveries |
| 5 Connected | 15 | The scrapyard's offers and chop orders |

A rookie gets no calls at night.

**At rank 5, the scrapyard texts you an offer** when a car goes on your hook: two to four times the fee, depending on
the call. Press <kbd>Y</kbd> within 45 seconds and the car goes to the crusher instead. The offer is a few times the fee, most on an
insurance car. It's never offered on a breakdown (someone's in the cab) or a live seizure (a player's car). **It's a
crime.** The gate camera often catches your plate, more so on a car the impound was expecting (a police seizure or a
transfer), for 2 wanted stars. If the camera misses, a passer-by may still phone it in.
**If the lot was expecting the car, the police are always told**: the model, the plate, and that it was last seen
entering the scrapyard. The car doesn't visibly crush: it's placed on the crusher and fades.

Delivering every kind of call at least once earns the **Tow Mater** achievement (see [Achievements and leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/)).

## Tips

- Stay in the driver's seat: getting out takes the card, and your <kbd>Y</kbd>, away.
- Night pays 25% more, but you need rank 2 before you get night calls.
- On a gang call, keep out of the crew's sight.
- Hook a parked car before the owner reaches it.
- Drive gently with a car on: righting the truck costs 10% of the fee every time.
- You can't hear the radio while you're wanted.
