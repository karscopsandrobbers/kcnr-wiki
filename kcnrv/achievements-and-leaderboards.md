Both live in the <kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard. Achievements are yours to collect. The leaderboards rank everybody.

## Achievements

**Dashboard > Achievements** lists every one you have earned and the date and time you earned it. Ones you haven't
earned aren't listed, so the page grows as you play.

When you earn one, a banner shows along the bottom of your screen, **chat tells the whole server** ("Kar has completed a
Time achievement", with its name), and it is posted to the Discord feed. Goals show the **Goal complete** card at the
top right instead of the banner, but the server is told all the same.

| Kind | What earns it |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | The four first goals: put $1,000 together, own a vehicle, reach rank 2, buy a house. See [Your first hour](../your-first-hour/) |
| Time | 5 minutes of play; 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15 and 20 hours; 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 days; 1 month |
| Crime | 1, 5, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 crimes committed. They are checked when you log in, so they arrive then, without a banner |
| Job | **Showroom condition**: bring a repo car back with nothing off the fee. **Simeon's Favourite**: bring one back from every tier. **Certified Gigolo**: be paid for every kind of pimp job. **Tow Mater**: deliver every kind of tow call |
| Game unlock | **Link Discord**: link your Discord account. See [Chat and Discord](../chat-and-discord/) |
| Secret | A few, and the game doesn't say what they are. Finding them is the point |

- **Only the goals pay.** The cash and XP come from the goal, not from the achievement, and each pays once.
- **Play time and crimes count across every team.** The 5-minute one comes soon after you start, and the longer ones
  land as you pass each mark.
- **24 hours of play also makes you a regular**, which is announced to everyone and posted to Discord.
- See [Simeon's Repo Jobs](../repo-jobs/), [Pimping (Working Girls)](../the-pimp/) and [Tow dispatch](../tow-dispatch/) for the three job ones.

## Leaderboards

**Dashboard > Leaderboards** has four pages:

| Page | What it ranks |
| --- | --- |
| This Week | What each player has added since the week began |
| Last Week | The week that just finished, as it ended |
| All Time | Every week since the server opened |
| Street Racing | The fastest lap on each race track |

### The counters

Each of the first three pages has a **tab for every counter**, and each tab shows the **top 25**. A player only appears
on a tab once they have moved that counter, so a quiet week shows only the people who played. Times show as a clock,
distances and fuel in your own units, and money in green dollars.

| Area | What is counted |
| --- | --- |
| You | Play time, kills, deaths, crimes committed and attempted |
| Crime | Robberies completed and the money taken, heists pulled off, robberies nobody saw, Gruppe Sechs trucks robbed, shoplifts and times caught, pursuits escaped, evasions, witnesses silenced, times a crime was reported |
| Law | Arrests, tickets issued and collected and the money, people handcuffed, searches (the same person counts once in ten minutes), robbers caught with the take, plate-reader hits, pursuit assists, scenes opened, and the police's good and dirty marks |
| Being caught | Times handcuffed, ticketed, arrested or taken down, tickets paid and the money paid |
| Gangs | Gang crimes stopped, gang members put down (up to 20 an hour count), money made selling to the gangs' corners, dealers shaken down |
| Jobs | Missions completed and the money earned from them, tow, repo and pimp jobs done, fires put out, scrapyard cars sold and what they earned |
| Racing | Races won, when somebody else was really racing |
| Trucking | Runs, petrol runs, distance, XP, money earned, perfect parks |
| Fishing | Fish caught, trophies, money earned, pots hauled |
| Fuel | Fuel bought at the pumps, for a car or a jerrycan, and what it cost with the tax |
| Business | Business income, supply runs, scrap hauls, promotions, defends, car washes, jobs done and money earned from them |
| Drugs | Money made selling drugs on the street, drug doses cooked, overdoses brought round (medics), positive drug tests (police) |
| Luck | Lottery jackpots won and total winnings, [animals hunted](../wildlife/) |

Only **counters** are ranked, never a "best". The biggest fish or your best lap time aren't on these pages; lap times are
on the Street Racing page.

### The week

A week runs **Sunday to Saturday** in real time, and turns over at **midnight UTC going into Sunday**. It can turn a
few minutes late. Everything on **This Week** starts again from zero, so a new player can
top a board. A week's figure is only what you added since it began.

When the week turns, **chat announces the winners** of arrests, robbery takings, missions completed and searches, and tells
you every board is back to zero. The same lines go to the Discord feed. The top 25 of the week that closed are kept, and
that is what **Last Week** shows.

### Street Racing

One tab per built track, with the fastest lap first. Each shows the top ten, the driver, the lap time and the car, and
says whether the track is a sprint or a number of laps, and its length in kilometres (always kilometres, whatever your
measurement setting is). **Each driver has one time per track**: a faster lap replaces your old one. A track appears once
somebody has set a time on it. See [Racing](../racing/).
