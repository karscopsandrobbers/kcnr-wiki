## When you can start

You must be a civilian and not on another job. The first job opens at civilian level 1, and the harder rungs open with
civilian level. The early jobs need no vehicle; **Client pickup** needs a car with a spare seat, and **VIP Escort** a car
with two passenger seats.

## How to start

There are three contacts: on the Vanilla Unicorn corner, behind Bahama Mamas, and on Orchardville Ave in La Mesa. Their
blips are on the map all day, faded by day and bright from 19:00 to 06:00 game time. Walk up and press <kbd>Y</kbd>:

- **Ask for work**: he picks a job from what your level has opened;
- **Help (name)**: join a job somebody else is already running;
- the whole ladder, with what locks each rung.

## How it works

1. He names the street and the fee. The corner is 500 m to 1.8 km away. Go and deal with it.
2. When the work's done, press <kbd>Y</kbd> to ring him, or he rings you after 15 seconds. The cash lands as the call
   ends.
3. He offers the next job on the phone, usually the next rung if your level opens it. <kbd>Y</kbd> takes it,
   <kbd>H</kbd> turns it down.

## The ladder

| Job | Civilian level | What you do | Fails if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trouble on the corner | 1 | A man is hassling one of the girls. He turns on anyone who comes close or aims at him. Pull a gun and he usually runs. | She's killed |
| Collect the earnings | 3, night only | Three corners. <kbd>E</kbd> on each girl and she counts the money out. Almost half the corners have a mugger who has to go first. | Any of the three is killed |
| Rival on the block | 5 | Another pimp, his girl and one or two men. Get close and the whole corner comes at you. Only he has to go, and a gun scares him off about half the time. | Time, or your death |
| Client pickup | 8 | Pick her up and drive her to the client. Somebody has to stay near the door for the booking. Half the time the client won't pay; beat him or pull a gun to get it. Then take her home. | She's killed |
| Hold the turf | 11 | Two girls on a corner, and three waves of attackers (3, 3 and 4). | Both girls are killed |
| VIP Escort | 15 | Collect him, collect her, and drive to a hotel. Deal with whatever comes out, then take him home. | He dies |

**A VIP night** can go several ways: he storms out angry (two punches calm him; 75% of the fee), she runs (catch her
within 45 seconds; 60%), he comes out bleeding (hospital first; 60%), or she defended herself (get her back safe; 90%).
On a quiet night, more often than not cars of hitters chase you home: stop and they come on foot, get 320 m clear and
they're gone, **kill them all and he tips $2,400 to $5,600**. A quiet night with no ambush sometimes tips $800 to
$2,200.

## Pay

The fee is the base plus $0.30 a metre from the contact to the first stop, plus 1% for every civilian level, up to 50%.

| Job | Base | Civilian XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trouble on the corner | $800 | 50 |
| Collect the earnings | $1,600 | 80 |
| Rival on the block | $2,400 | 110 |
| Client pickup | $2,000 (70% if the client never paid) | 120 |
| Hold the turf | $3,600 | 160 |
| VIP Escort | $6,000, plus tips, less any share for a bad night | 220 |

**For example:** Trouble on the corner 1 km out, at level 1, pays $1,111. A VIP job 1.5 km out pays $7,418 at level
15, and $9,675 at level 50.

Between jobs there's a 15-second wait, plus 10 seconds for each one you run back to back, up to 2 minutes. After a
failure it's 45 seconds.

## With a friend

Every job takes two people. A friend joins three ways: your [party](../groups-and-parties/) (`/party`) gets a card
the moment you take it; any civilian who comes near the scene gets a card (`/jm` joins); or the contact's **Help**
row. The pot grows by half the fee and is split evenly, so **two of you get 75% each**, and each gets the job's full
XP. The fee is set by the level of whoever took the job, so the friend needs no level at all. `/mission leave` takes
you out.

## Tips

- Ring him as soon as the work's done. Nothing is paid until the call ends.
- *Collect the earnings* is only offered from 19:00 to 06:00 game time. A game hour is two real minutes.
- Bring the right car: one spare seat for a client pickup, two for the VIP.
- On a VIP night, killing the hitters pays the big tip. Outrunning them doesn't.

Being paid for all six jobs earns the **Certified Gigolo** achievement.
