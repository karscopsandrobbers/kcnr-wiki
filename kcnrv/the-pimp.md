## When you can start

You must be a civilian and not on another job, in a stick-up, cuffed, being arrested, in jail or robbing: a contact
won't talk to anyone else. The first job opens at civilian level 1, and the harder rungs open with
civilian level. The early jobs need no vehicle; **Client pickup** needs a car with a spare seat, and **VIP Escort** a car
with two passenger seats.

## How to start

There are three contacts: on the Vanilla Unicorn corner, behind Bahama Mamas, and on Orchardville Ave in La Mesa. Their
blips are on the map all day, faded by day and bright from 19:00 to 06:00 game time. Walk up and press <kbd>Y</kbd>:

- **Ask for work**: he picks a job from what your level has opened;
- **Help (name)**: join a job somebody else is already running, if it's still open and has room;
- the whole ladder, with what locks each rung: a rung shows **Level N**, or **Night only** when it's daytime.

Which of the three men stands on which corner is shuffled every time the server restarts.

**Sweet Lou's call.** Below level 10, Sweet Lou may phone you with Trouble on the corner (see
[Your first hour](../your-first-hour/)); press <kbd>Y</kbd> on the card after the call. The first time, he wants it
**quiet**: put the man down with your fists or a blade, or point a gun at him and let him run, but nobody in the crew
fires a shot. Do it that way and he tips **$300 to $600** on top of the fee. One shot and he tells you the tip is gone.
He also tells you how to bring a friend (below, With a friend).

## How it works

1. He names the street and the fee. Go and deal with it. **Every job has a clock**: time for the
   drive out, plus more for the harder kinds of job. Run out and the job fails.
2. When the work's done, press <kbd>Y</kbd> to ring him, or he rings you after 15 seconds. The cash lands as the call
   ends. If the call drops, the 15 seconds start again and you're told to ring him again.
3. He offers the next job on the phone. Until your wait is over the bar says he's setting it up, and the key does
   nothing. Then he holds the job for a short while: <kbd>Y</kbd> takes it, <kbd>H</kbd> (shown once he's ready)
   turns it down. The offer goes to **everyone in the crew**. It's the rung above the one you just did, if your level
   opens it; at the top of the ladder it's a random one other than the one you just did. It still needs your level, the
   night for night-only jobs, and the wait to be over.

On screen you'll see bars for **TIME LEFT**, **YOUR CUT** (your share given the crew so far) and **CREW** once anybody
joins, and on a VIP night **ENEMIES** (red, during the ambush), **BLEEDING** and a countdown for the booking. After the
call, chat tells you what you did, where, how far and how long, then "Your cut of a crew of N" from the pot and your
XP, and a "JOB DONE" banner shows. Every enemy you fight has a red dot on the map.

**The horn (VIP Escort).** If you're the driver and have been parked near the stop for a few seconds while
the job waits for him or her, a "Sound the horn for them" button appears. Press it and the pull-up point moves to where
your car is, and they walk out to you.

## The ladder

| Job | Civilian level | What you do | Fails if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trouble on the corner | 1 | A man is hassling one of the girls. He turns on the first of you who gets close, or the first to aim a gun at him: usually with fists, otherwise a bottle or a knife. Pull a gun and he usually runs, though now and then one stands his ground. | She's killed |
| Collect the earnings | 3, night only | Three corners. <kbd>E</kbd> on each girl and she counts the money out over 4 seconds. She hands over nothing while a mugger is on his feet, and many corners have one. | Any of the three is killed |
| Rival on the block | 5 | Another pimp, his girl and one or two men. Get close or aim a gun and the whole corner comes at you. Only he has to go, and a gun sometimes scares him off. Hurting his girl changes nothing. | Time, or your death |
| Client pickup | 8 | Pull up next to her and stop; she takes the front seat. Drive her to the client's door and stop there. The booking lasts 45 seconds, and somebody has to stay near the door (you get a few seconds' grace if you drive off). Sometimes the client won't pay: some fight, the rest run; beat him or pull a gun to get paid in full. Then take her home. | She's killed, or you drive off and leave her |
| Hold the turf | 11 | Two girls on a corner. Three waves come at you on foot, each a few seconds after the last one is down. | Both girls are killed |
| VIP Escort | 15 | Collect him, collect her, and drive to a hotel. Stay near the hotel while they're inside. Deal with whatever comes out, then take him home. | He dies, you lose him, you drive off and leave her, or he bleeds out |

**Every job also fails for you** if time runs out, you die, or you walk away from it with `/mission leave`. It pays you
nothing and you wait 45 seconds before the next one, even if you were the one who left. If anyone is still in, the rest
of the crew carry on.

**Jobs open to the crew until the fight starts.** A friend can join a job until the trouble begins (the man turns, the
corner turns, the first wave arrives), and again only until the work is done and just the call is left. Only civilians
can join.

**A VIP night.** Collect him (a doorway or the front step of a house), then her (a kerb corner, or sometimes a house),
and drive to a hotel a way on (a real motel, apartment block or hotel, named on the map). Watch them go in, then wait. **How long they're inside tells you how the night
went**: up to a minute and a half on a quiet night (the bar emptying means they're on their way down), much shorter if
something went wrong. What comes out:

| What comes out | How often | What to do | Share of the fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A quiet night | Most often | Take him home | 100%, and tips |
| He storms out angry | Fairly often | He may swing, and may come out undressed. Two punches calm him (or enough damage, or 25 seconds). She gets in by herself | 75% |
| She runs | Sometimes | She runs off. Catch her inside 45 seconds and she gets in; let the clock run out and she's lost | 60% |
| He comes out bleeding | Sometimes | He bleeds slowly (a BLEEDING bar shows). Get him to the **nearest hospital** first, then home. If he bleeds out the job fails | 60% |
| She defended herself | Rarely | Get her back safe | 90% |

**Only a quiet night can be followed.** On a quiet night, often, a few cars of armed hitters appear behind you on your own road, partway through the drive home. They only get out once you've
**stopped near one of their cars**; drive off and they get back in. Stop and they come on
foot; get well clear of all of them and they're gone. An ENEMIES bar counts how many are left. **Kill them
all and he tips $2,400 to $5,600**. A quiet night with no ambush sometimes tips $800 to $2,200. Tips show as their own green line.

## Pay

The fee is the base plus $0.30 a metre from the contact to the first stop, plus 1% for every civilian level, up to 50%.

| Job | Base | Civilian XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trouble on the corner | $800 | 50 |
| Collect the earnings | $1,600 | 80 |
| Rival on the block | $2,400 | 110 |
| Client pickup | $2,000 (70% if the client never paid) | 120 |
| Hold the turf | $3,600 | 160 |
| VIP Escort | $6,000, plus tips, less any share for a bad night | 220 |

**For example:** Trouble on the corner 1 km out, at level 1, pays $1,111. A VIP job 1.5 km out pays $7,418 at level
15, and $9,675 at level 50.

Between jobs there's a short wait that grows a little for each one you run back to back, and resets if you stop for a while. Every paid crew member gets the wait. After a failure it's 45
seconds.

## With a friend

Every job takes two people. A friend joins three ways: your [party](../groups-and-parties/) (`/party`) gets a card
the moment you take it; any civilian who comes near the scene gets a card (`/jm` joins); or the contact's **Help**
row. The pot grows by half the fee and is split evenly, so **two of you get 75% each**, and each gets the job's full
XP. The fee is set by the level of whoever took the job, so the friend needs no level at all. `/mission leave` takes
you out. The scene's card goes to any civilian near wherever the job currently is, and the circle
follows the job from leg to leg. A friend who joins too late to be cut in sees "You joined too late to be cut in". A job
with two of you paid counts as a job **with another player** for the [work board](../your-first-hour/); a party card is
the same card as a party invite, so you only see one at a time (see [Groups and parties](../groups-and-parties/)).

## Tips

- Ring him as soon as the work's done. Nothing is paid until the call ends.
- *Collect the earnings* is only offered from 19:00 to 06:00 game time. A game hour is two real minutes.
- Bring the right car: one spare seat for a client pickup, two for the VIP.
- On a VIP night, killing the hitters pays the big tip. Outrunning them doesn't.

Being paid for all six jobs earns the **Certified Gigolo** achievement (see [Achievements and leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/)). Only jobs paid since 2 October 2026 count
towards it.

If you leave a job, you still wait 45 seconds before taking another.
