## The runs

A truck goes out every 12 real minutes at most: one at a time, or two at once with 4 or more players online. It leaves
the Union Depository with one or two cash duffels and calls at banks and the jeweller, where a guard walks in and comes
back with a briefcase or a cash bag. Pacific Standard, Blaine County and Vangelico always send a big bag, and Pacific
Standard sometimes a bag of gold. The truck gets **one escort car** once its cargo reaches $40,000, **two** at $80,000.

## Attacking one

Shooting it, ramming it, killing a guard or setting off an explosive near it makes it run: it speeds off through red
lights and skips its next errand, its escorts chase anyone near, and every guard shows as a red dot on your map. The
glass and the tyres are bulletproof.

**It's stopped** when nobody's left to drive it, its engine or fuel tank is shot out, or it's held still for 8 seconds
while under attack. Then the crew get out and fight with rifles and shotguns.

**Backup:** a guard radios for help (2 seconds). If no officer reaches the truck 45 seconds after the call (20 with no
police online), a car of four armed guards drives in. No guard alive to call, no backup.

## Getting into the back

- **Blow the rear doors**: a sticky bomb on them, or a grenade, rocket, mine or pipe bomb at the back. A molotov doesn't
  work.
- **Or drill the lock**: the truck standing, with nobody in the cab. If it moves, the drilling is lost.

Then press <kbd>E</kbd> at the back to take the cargo out one piece at a time, into your hands (the depot's duffels
come out last). <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> puts a piece down; <kbd>E</kbd> at a car boot stows it.

## Opening it, or not

Cargo is **sealed**. Open a briefcase or bag with a blade or crowbar in your hand, or a drill in your pockets: 8
seconds standing still, from your inventory or with <kbd>Y</kbd> where it lies. Cut a duffel open on the ground with a
blade. About one piece in five hides a **dye pack**, which spoils half the cash. A gold bag gives you its bars. Opening
cargo is Possession of Stolen Property if somebody phones it in. **Or sell it sealed** to the G6 insider: see
[Selling stolen goods](../selling-stolen-goods/).

## What you're charged with

| What you did | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Attacked a truck | Attempted Armed Robbery, 3 stars |
| Took cargo out of it | One more star (Armed Robbery) |
| Drove the truck off | Theft, 2 stars |

You're charged if an officer within 150 m sees you, or a passer-by within 80 m phones it in.

## Pay

| Cargo | Worth |
| --- | --- |
| Briefcase | $5,000 to $10,000 |
| Cash bag | $12,000 to $22,000 |
| Big bank bag (Pacific Standard, Blaine County, Vangelico) | $40,000 to $70,000 |
| Depot cash duffel | $10,000 to $18,000, one or two a truck |
| Gold bag (Pacific Standard) | 4 to 7 gold bars |

There's no XP for the attack itself; the insider pays 10 Robbing XP a piece.

## Tips

- Selling sealed to the insider never pays less than opening it yourself, and he already knows which pieces carry dye.
- Blow the whole truck up and the briefcases and gold are thrown clear, but the bags of money burn.
- Kill a guard carrying a case out of a bank and he drops it; hit him and he runs.
- Buy the insider's tip and wait in his circle: the truck drives in.

**For officers:** every run is on your map, and you can escort one. See [Police](../police/).
