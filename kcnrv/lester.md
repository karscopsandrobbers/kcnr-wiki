## Where

His house in Murrieta Heights. A light-green "Lester" marker shows on your radar only when you're close, and his front
door is open.

## How it works

Press <kbd>Y</kbd> on him. He talks to anybody, officers included, but **sells to civilians only**, for cash in hand.
His menu has three rants (His adventures, Conspiracies and The Epsilon Program; walk more than 15 m away and he stops)
and his wares, each price green if you have the cash and red if you don't. He sells you one hacking device at a time:
come back when it burns out.

## What to buy

- **The hacking device** is the cheap way in: every hack a step easier, until it burns out (one hack in four).
- **The laptop** is two steps and never breaks. If you carry both, only the laptop counts, so don't carry the device
  as well.
- **USB drives** only work in the laptop: buy them once you own one.
- **Thermite** does nothing yet: no job uses it.

All four are contraband, so a police search takes them. See [Banks and hacking](../banks-and-hacking/) for what each
step does.
