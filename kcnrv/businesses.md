You need **total level 5**. A lease is paid up front, from cash and then bank. Anybody in a holder's
[party](../groups-and-parties/) can crew their business jobs, with no level.

## The businesses

| Business | Kind | Slots | Gang ground | Lease a day (first term) | One slot's game day, at full |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car Wash, Strawberry | Car wash | 4 | Families | $2,500 | $200 |
| Car Wash, Little Seoul | Car wash | 3 | Kkangpae | $2,500 | $200 |
| Breaking Bad Scrapyard | Scrapyard | 4 | none | $3,000 | $240 |
| Tequilala | Nightclub | 4 | none | $3,500 | $280 |
| Bahama Mamas | Nightclub | 5 | none | $4,000 | $320 |
| Vanilla Unicorn | Strip club | 5 | Families | $4,000 | $320 |

The shops (24/7s, Ammu-Nation, gas stations and the rest) belong to the city and can't be bought or leased.

**Where:** City Hall's **Business leases**, `/businesses` from anywhere, and a green turning dollar sign at each
business. Stand on it on foot and press <kbd>E</kbd>; collecting and starting jobs only work there.

## Leasing

Choose **For** (you, or your group) and a **Term** of 7, 14, 21 or 30 real days, then **Lease**. You hold one slot
yourself, two from reputation 3, and a group can hold three; each holder gets one slot per business. A lease runs
whether you're online or not.

| Lease, per real day | 1st term | 2nd term | 3rd term on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car washes | $2,500 | $3,250 | $3,500 |
| Scrapyard | $3,000 | $3,900 | $4,200 |
| Tequilala | $3,500 | $4,550 | $4,900 |
| Bahama Mamas, Vanilla Unicorn | $4,000 | $5,200 | $5,600 |

Each term in a row costs more but earns more for staying: **+5%** in the 2nd term, **+10%** from the 3rd. Leasing the
same business again within 30 days of your last lease there ending counts as the next term. **Renewing** opens in the
last 7 days of a term (the last half of a 7-day lease), and the new days go on the end. **Selling back** (Sell Back,
twice) returns half your unused days into your bank. When a lease runs out, what's left in the safe, less 6%, goes into
your next paycheck.

## What a slot earns

Every game day (48 real minutes) each slot gets the business's own earnings, scaled by its **condition** and **level**,
plus an equal share of what players spent there: wash fees, club drinks, and at the yard a quarter of the metal value
of every car sold.

- **Condition** (Supplies, Yard space or Popularity) runs 0 to 100 and drops 2 points a real hour. A slot earns a
  quarter of its money at 0 and all of it at 100. A business job puts 30 back.
- **Level** belongs to the business: 5 XP for each player customer, 40 for each job, up through levels 2 to 5 at 400,
  1,200, 3,000 and 6,000 XP. Each level above 1 adds 10% to every slot.

Condition, level and customer money are shared by every holder, so anyone's job helps them all.

## The safe

Collect it at the dollar sign, within 4 m: into your cash, or the group bank for a group slot. 6% business tax comes
off as the money leaves. **Leave it 24 real hours and the slot earns at 60%** until somebody collects; you're warned at
12, 18 and 24 hours. At login, chat tells you what each of your safes holds and how many days are left on each lease.

## Business jobs

The holder starts a job at the dollar sign; only their party can join, with <kbd>Y</kbd> on the card within 30
seconds. Four people at most, one job at a time per business, and not while you're on another mission. Afterwards
the crew waits **12 minutes**, and a failed job costs the same.

| Job | At | What you do | Time | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resupply | Washes, clubs | Drive a van to a supplier 400 m to 3 km away, load for 5 s, bring it back | 15 min | $4,000 |
| Scrap Haul | Scrapyard | Load a truck at the yard, take it to the docks | 15 min | $4,800 |
| Promotion | Clubs | A promo van stops 5 s at three shops | 15 min | $4,000 |
| Defend | Gang ground, after it's smashed | Three waves of the gang | 10 min | $3,000 |
| Crush Evidence | Scrapyard | Fetch a gang's car and crush it | 20 min | $5,000 to $8,000 from the gang |

The pot grows half again for each extra member and is split evenly: on a $4,000 job, one gets $4,000, two $3,000 each,
three $2,667, four $2,500. You're paid in full if you were in it for a minute, half if it was quicker.

## Reputation

Your small-business reputation (0 to 5) is your **Business** skill: 40 XP for each business job (crew too) and 10 for
each safe collected. Reputation 1 to 5 come at Business levels 2, 4, 7, 11 and 16.

| Reputation | You get |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10% off car washes and club drinks |
| 2 | Your own jobs pay you 15% more, and your cooldown is 20% shorter |
| 3 | Non-holders on your jobs earn 25% more; the business gets 15% more XP from your jobs; a second slot |
| 4 | Your own slots earn 15% more (group slots don't) |
| 5 | Your slot's money can go to your paycheck instead of the safe: no trip to collect, no 60% rate |

## Gangs and protection

The Strawberry wash and the Vanilla Unicorn are on Families ground; the Little Seoul wash on Kkangpae ground. While
the holders pay, **12% of each slot's day goes to the gang**, and a collector walks in each game day if somebody's
near.

Each slot votes **Pay** or **Refuse**; more refusals than payers refuses the gang, and a tie pays. Killing the
collector counts as refusing. After a refusal the gang comes 4 to 12 real minutes later, with a warning: with a player
within 150 m, three of them walk in with bats and smash the place for 40 seconds, and dropping all three in time stops
it. If they win, or nobody's near, the place is **smashed up**: condition drops 20, the police are called, and it earns
nothing of its own and gets no customers until **Defend** is run or the holders vote to pay.

## Rivals

The two washes are rivals, and so are the three clubs; the yard has none. A hit is a job you start at your own dollar
sign, against a business you don't hold a slot in; a business that's been hit is left alone for 6 real hours.

| Hit | Against | What you do | They lose | Crime | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grease the Soap | A wash | 8 s on foot at its lane | 25 supplies | Vandalism | $6,000 |
| Smash the Bar | An open club | 8 s at its till | 20 popularity, and it shuts for a while | Vandalism, 1 star | $7,000 |
| Arson | A wash or club | Douse the marked spots from a jerrycan, light them with a molotov or flare | Condition while it burns, and no customers | Arson, 2 stars | $10,000 |
| Rob a Rival | An open club | Hold up its till | 15 popularity | The hold-up's own | $6,000 plus the till |
| Hijack a Delivery | A wash or club with its Resupply van out | Take the van and drive it home | Their delivery; yours gets +30 | Grand Theft Auto, 1 star | $8,000 |

A hit business's holders are told what happened, never who did it.

## The scrapyard

No gang and no rival. Every car sold there uses yard space, and **Scrap Haul** frees 30 points of it. **Crush
Evidence**: each of the crew gets a 150 m circle on the map, the car appears once one of you is within 200 m, and you
drive it to the yard and hold it there 5 seconds. One time in four the gate camera reads the plate (Accessory, 2 stars,
for whoever drove last), a police plate reader that catches you at the wheel is 1 star, and an arrest within 60 m of
the car ends the job unpaid.

## Tips

- Jobs are your upkeep: one job covers about 15 real hours of wear.
- Collect at least once a day. Even opening an empty safe resets the 60% clock.
- The scrapyard pays no gang and has no rivals.
- **Rundown, then Recent** shows what's happened lately, including hits on you.
- Your safe can't be robbed.
