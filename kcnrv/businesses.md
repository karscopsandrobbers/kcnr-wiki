You need **total level 5**. A lease is paid up front, from cash and then bank. Anybody in a holder's
[party](../groups-and-parties/) can crew their business jobs, with no level.

## The businesses

| Business | Kind | Slots | Gang ground | Lease a day (first term) | One slot's game day, at full |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car Wash, Strawberry | Car wash | 4 | Families | $2,500 | $200 |
| Car Wash, Little Seoul | Car wash | 3 | Kkangpae | $2,500 | $200 |
| Breaking Bad Scrapyard | Scrapyard | 4 | none | $3,000 | $240 |
| Tequilala | Nightclub | 4 | none | $3,500 | $280 |
| Bahama Mamas | Nightclub | 5 | none | $4,000 | $320 |
| Vanilla Unicorn | Strip club | 5 | Families | $4,000 | $320 |

The shops (24/7s, Ammu-Nation, gas stations and the rest) belong to the city and can't be bought or leased.

**Where:** City Hall's **Business leases**, `/businesses` from anywhere, and a green turning dollar sign at each
business. Stand on it on foot and press <kbd>E</kbd>; collecting and starting jobs only work there.

## The business menu

<kbd>E</kbd> at the dollar sign opens the menu. What it shows depends on whether you hold a slot.

| If you hold a slot | If you don't |
| --- | --- |
| **Your Slot:** Collect, the safe, where it pays into, Renew, Sell Back | **Lease a Slot:** for you or for your group, 7, 14, 21 or 30 days, the price and who holds each slot |
| **Jobs:** each one, with why you can't start it if you can't | **Jobs:** what the business offers |
| **Rundown:** condition, level, today, this week, the city's take, jobs by whom, and Recent | **Protection:** the gang, its cut and the votes |
| **Protection:** the gang, its cut, the votes and your own vote | **Your Reputation** |
| **Slots:** who holds each slot | |
| **Your Reputation** | |

A price is green if you can pay it and red if you can't. **Lease** is greyed out with the reason why. Leasing,
renewing, selling back and collecting each put up a big message, and opening the safe plays the heist cash-grab.

## Leasing

Choose **For** (you, or your group) and a **Term** of 7, 14, 21 or 30 real days, then **Lease**. You hold one slot
yourself, two from reputation 3, and a group can hold three; each holder gets one slot per business. A lease runs
whether you're online or not.

- **A group and its members can't both hold a slot at one business.** If your group holds one, you can't lease one
  yourself there, and if a member of your group already does, the group can't.
- **A group's ranks decide who does what.** Leasing and renewing for the group needs the rank allowed to **lease
  business slots** (it's the group bank that pays). Collecting the safe, starting jobs, voting on protection and
  selling back need the rank allowed to **manage business slots**. See [Groups and parties](../groups-and-parties/).

| Lease, per real day | 1st term | 2nd term | 3rd term on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car washes | $2,500 | $3,250 | $3,500 |
| Scrapyard | $3,000 | $3,900 | $4,200 |
| Tequilala | $3,500 | $4,550 | $4,900 |
| Bahama Mamas, Vanilla Unicorn | $4,000 | $5,200 | $5,600 |

Each term in a row costs more but earns more for staying: **+5%** in the 2nd term, **+10%** from the 3rd. Leasing the
same business again within 30 days of your last lease there ending counts as the next term. **Renewing** opens in the
last 7 days of a term (the last half of a 7-day lease), and the new days go on the end. **Selling back** (Sell Back,
twice) returns half your unused days, at your lease's own daily rate, into your bank (or the group bank for a group's
slot). **You're warned when a lease is running out**: at 7, 3 and 1 days left, but only the marks shorter than the
term, so a 7-day lease warns at 3 and 1. When a lease runs out or is sold back, what's left in the safe, less 6%, is
paid out first: into your next paycheck, or the group bank for a group's slot.

## What a slot earns

Every game day (48 real minutes) each slot gets the business's own earnings, scaled by its **condition** and **level**,
plus an equal share of what players spent there: wash fees, club drinks, and at the yard a quarter of the metal value
of every car sold.

- **Condition** (Supplies, Yard space or Popularity) runs 0 to 100, **starts at 60** and slowly drops as real time passes. A
  slot earns a quarter of its money at 0 and all of it at 100. A business job puts a good chunk back. Player customers move it
  too: a car wash uses up supplies with every wash, the yard fills with every car sold, and a club gains popularity with every drink.
- **Level** belongs to the business: 5 XP for each player customer, 40 for each job, up through levels 2 to 5 at 400,
  1,200, 3,000 and 6,000 XP. Each level above 1 adds 10% to every slot.

Condition, level and customer money are shared by every holder, so anyone's job helps them all. Each holding also keeps
its own record: what it earned, what you collected, the days paid, the player customers it had, your own jobs,
protection paid, renewals and any refund.

**The cars you see pulling in are decoration.** One drives up at a time while a player is nearby, every so often, and slower as condition falls. They show how busy the place looks and
**earn nothing**; there are none while the place is smashed up or burning. On gang ground, some of them are the gang's own.

## The safe

Collect it at the dollar sign, within 4 m: into your cash, or the group bank for a group slot. 6% business tax comes
off as the money leaves. **Leave it 24 real hours and the slot earns at 60%** until somebody collects; you're warned at
12, 18 and 24 hours. At login, chat tells you what each of your safes holds and how many days are left on each lease. **Only your own slot
can be sent to your paycheck** (see Reputation); a group's slot is always collected, into the group bank.

## Business jobs

The holder starts a job at the dollar sign; only their party can join, with <kbd>Y</kbd> on the card within 30
seconds. Four people at most, one job at a time per business, and not while you're on another mission. Afterwards
the crew waits **12 minutes**, and a failed job costs the same.

| Job | At | What you do | Time | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resupply | Washes, clubs | Drive a van to a supplier across town, load for 5 s, bring it back | 15 min | $4,000 |
| Scrap Haul | Scrapyard | Load a truck at the yard, take it to the docks | 15 min | $4,800 |
| Promotion | Clubs | A promo van stops 5 s at three shops | 15 min | $4,000 |
| Defend | Gang ground, after it's smashed | Three waves of the gang, each bigger than the last | 10 min | $3,000 |
| Crush Evidence | Scrapyard | Fetch a gang's car and crush it | 20 min | $5,000 to $8,000 from the gang |

The pot grows half again for each extra member and is split evenly: on a $4,000 job, one gets $4,000, two $3,000 each,
three $2,667, four $2,500. You're paid in full if you were in it for a minute, half if it was quicker.

- **Resupply:** a car wash's van goes to a **gas station** and unloads at its bay; a club's truck goes to a **liquor
  store**. You bring the load home to the wash lane or the club door.
- **Promotion:** the three shops are a short drive from the club, picked from 24/7s, liquor stores,
  clothing stores, bars, tattoo parlours and barbers.
- **A stop counts** when the job's vehicle is right at it. **Only the crew counts at the wheel**: with
  anybody else driving it, no stop counts and the hint tells you to get it back.
- **It fails** if the vehicle is wrecked or the clock runs out. Nobody is paid, and the crew still waits the 12 minutes.
- **While the place is smashed up** every other job is refused: Defend is the only one offered until it's put right.
- **A won Defend** puts back less condition than other jobs and keeps the gang away for 3 game days, about 2.4 real
  hours. The enemies carry pistols, micro SMGs, bats and machetes, and each shows as a red dot on the map.
- **On screen**, a job shows the next stop as a blip with a route and a marker on the ground, and bars for the time
  left, your pay, the crew (always the last bar) and, in a defence, the enemies left.

## Reputation

Your small-business reputation (0 to 5) is your **Business** skill: 40 XP for each business job (crew too) and 10 for
each safe collected. Reputation 1 to 5 come at Business levels 2, 4, 7, 11 and 16.

| Reputation | You get |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10% off car washes and club drinks |
| 2 | Your own jobs pay you 15% more, and your cooldown is 20% shorter |
| 3 | Non-holders on your jobs earn 25% more; the business gets 15% more XP from your jobs; a second slot |
| 4 | Your own slots earn 15% more (group slots don't) |
| 5 | Your own slot's money can go to your paycheck instead of the safe, less the 6% business tax: no trip to collect, no 60% rate, and no income tax on top (it was already taxed). A group's slot can't |

## Gangs and protection

The Strawberry wash and the Vanilla Unicorn are on Families ground; the Little Seoul wash on Kkangpae ground. While
the holders pay, **12% of each slot's day goes to the gang**, and a collector walks in each game day if somebody's
near.

Each slot votes **Pay** or **Refuse**; more refusals than payers refuses the gang, and a tie pays. Killing the
collector counts as refusing. **The collector** waits 15 seconds at the dollar sign for the cut. It's taken whether
or not anybody sees him, and he can't be robbed.

After a refusal the gang comes a few minutes later, and **the holders are warned ahead**. With a
player nearby, three of them walk in with bats and smash the place, and dropping all
three in time stops it. If they win (or they're still fighting after a good while), if nobody's near, or if nobody gets to
the place in time, the place is **smashed up**: condition drops sharply, the police are called, and it earns
nothing of its own and gets no customers until **Defend** is run or the holders vote to pay. A smashed place also has
**wreckage** scattered round its dollar sign until it's put right, and that stays through a restart.

## Rivals

The two washes are rivals, and so are the three clubs; the yard has none. A hit is a job you start at your own dollar
sign, against a business you don't hold a slot in; a business that's been hit is left alone for 6 real hours.

| Hit | Against | What you do | They lose | Crime | Time | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grease the Soap | A wash | 8 s on foot at its lane | Supplies | Vandalism | 15 min | $6,000 |
| Smash the Bar | An open club | 8 s at its till | Popularity, and it shuts for a while | Vandalism, 1 star | 15 min | $7,000 |
| Arson | A wash or club | Douse the marked spots from a jerrycan, light them with a molotov or flare | Condition while it burns, and no customers | Arson, 2 stars | 15 min | $10,000 |
| Rob a Rival | An open club | Hold up its till | Popularity | The hold-up's own | 20 min | $6,000 plus the till |
| Hijack a Delivery | A wash or club with its Resupply van out | Take the van and drive it home | Their delivery; yours gets a boost | Grand Theft Auto, 1 star | 15 min | $8,000 |

A hit business's holders are told what happened, never who did it.

- **Who you can hit:** a business somebody holds, that you don't hold a slot in, and that isn't already burning. A shut
  club can't be Smashed or Robbed.
- **Arson** costs the rival condition at once and more every minute it burns, and it burns out after 10 minutes if
  nobody comes. The marked spots run along a car wash's lane or at the tills in a club. Pour petrol from a jerrycan until the orange markers turn green, **most of them**, then light
  it with a molotov or a flare. Molotovs and flares are on most Ammu-Nation shelves: see
  [Items and your pockets](../items/). The fire brigade is called and paid, a burning club shuts, nobody can pay for a
  wash while it burns, and the holders get a phone call from the manager when it's lit and when it's out. Each crew
  member who poured gets the 2 stars.
- **Hijack a Delivery** is only offered while a rival's Resupply van is on the road. Its holders are told when it sets
  off, your crew's map follows the van, and the rival crew can still wreck it.

## The scrapyard

No gang and no rival. Every car sold there uses yard space, and **Scrap Haul** frees 30 points of it. **Crush
Evidence**: each of the crew gets a 150 m circle on the map, the car appears once one of you is within 200 m, and you
drive it to the yard and hold it there 5 seconds. One time in four the gate camera reads the plate (Accessory, 2 stars,
for whoever drove last), a police plate reader that catches you at the wheel is 1 star, and an arrest within 60 m of
the car ends the job unpaid. The car is one of the gang's own models, left on the gang's ground. Hold it at the yard for
5 seconds and everybody is put out and it fades; the yard also gets a $400 fee that joins the customer money shared at
the next game day.

## Tips

- Jobs are your upkeep: one job covers about 15 real hours of wear.
- Collect at least once a day. Even opening an empty safe resets the 60% clock.
- The scrapyard pays no gang and has no rivals.
- **Rundown, then Recent** shows what's happened lately, including hits on you.
- **Business income, supply runs, scrap hauls, promotions, defences, washes, jobs done and job earnings** all have a
  place on the <kbd>F10</kbd> leaderboards (see [Achievements and leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/)).
- Your safe can't be robbed.
