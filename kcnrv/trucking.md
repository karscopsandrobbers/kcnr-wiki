## When you can start

- You must be a civilian, at any level, and not wanted.
- **Cargo:** a box truck (Benson, Biff, Mule, Pounder, Terbyte) carries the load itself; a tractor unit (Hauler,
  Packer, a Phantom) pulls a trailer.
- **Petrol:** you need a tractor unit.
- **No truck?** The board lends you one, for 10% off the distance pay. You can't start in a car that isn't a truck.

## How to start

- **Cargo boards:** the docks on Elysian Island, and Grapeseed. **Petrol:** the yellow Petrol Delivery board on the map.
- Drive or walk into a board's circle and the menu opens; <kbd>E</kbd> opens it again.
- **Cargo:** choose **Short haul** or **Long haul**, then **Begin Mission**.
- **Petrol:** choose **Begin Mission**, then pick a station. The list shows each station's tank, emptiest first.

## A cargo run

1. Billy at the Trucking Guild texts you the pickup: a police station, bank or car dealership, 300 m to 2 km away.
2. Drive into the pickup marker in your truck. A box truck loads for 5 seconds while you're held still; a tractor unit
   gets a trailer dropped behind it, so back up and hitch it.
3. Drive to the drop, a shop, bar, club, restaurant or the like: 1.5 to 3.5 km on for a short haul, 6 km or more for a
   long one.
4. Stop inside the drop marker and you're paid. Where a drop has a **loading bay**, back the load into the outline,
   stop and press <kbd>E</kbd> for a parking bonus.
5. Billy then offers another load near you. <kbd>H</kbd> turns it down.

## A petrol run

1. A tanker appears at the depot: you have about 30 seconds to back onto it.
2. Drive to the station and stop in its marker. Its tank takes up to 2,000 litres.
3. If the tanker is destroyed, or you arrive without it, nothing is delivered and nothing is paid.

Every gas station has a tank that fill-ups drain, and only petrol runs refill it. A station under 30% shows a yellow
blip; a dry one is grey and its pumps are closed.

## The clock

- **Cargo:** 3 to 8 minutes to reach the pickup, then a second for every 9 m to the drop, never more than 20 minutes.
- **Petrol:** 30 seconds to hitch, then a second for every 9 m to the station.

A run fails with no pay, and a 45-second wait, if the clock runs out, you die, your truck is destroyed, a fragile load
is badly damaged, or the tanker is destroyed. A trailer destroyed before you hitch it costs 50% of the distance pay; a
hitched cargo trailer destroyed on the way costs 30%, and the trailer bonus.

## Pay

| | Cargo run | Petrol run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distance pay | $500 plus $0.54 a metre | $0.40 a metre |
| Trailer still hitched at the end | +10% of the distance pay | +10% |
| The petrol | | $0.80 a litre at a full tank, up to $1.60 at an empty one, for up to 2,000 litres |
| Loading bay | Perfect +20%, Good +12%, Fair +5% | The same, where a station has one |
| Each special load (total level 4+) | +10 to 25% | |
| A borrowed truck | -10% of the distance pay | -10% |
| Civilian XP | 40, +12 a km, +20 a special load, +20/12/5 for parking | 40, +12 a km, +30 |
| Wait before the next run | 1 minute after a short haul, 2 after a long one | 2 minutes |

Distance is measured in a straight line, not the road you drove: for cargo, from where you took the job to the pickup
plus the pickup to the drop; for petrol, from the depot to the station.

**For example:** a short haul covering 3.5 km in all pays $2,390 and 82 XP. An 8 km long haul in a tractor that keeps
its trailer pays $5,302 and 136 XP. A 3 km petrol run to a station at 20% pays $4,200 and 106 XP.

## Getting better at it

There's no trucking rank: every run is civilian XP. At **total level 4**, about three cargo runs in four carry a
special load for more pay:

- **Illegal goods**: if a police officer spots you, you're charged with smuggling or trafficking.
- **Fragile goods**: if the truck or trailer is badly damaged, the run fails.
- **Zoo animals**: the trailer lurches on its own every 15 to 40 seconds.

## Tips

- Bring your own truck: a borrowed one costs 10% of the distance pay.
- A tractor earns the 10% trailer bonus, but only with the trailer still hitched when you arrive.
- Petrol pays best at the emptiest stations.
- Take Billy's next load at the drop instead of driving back to the depot.
- Long hauls pay more a run; short hauls get you back on the board sooner.
