**No level needed** for joining a team (not while you're wanted, and the police turn away anyone with unpaid
tickets), robbing [Vangelico](../the-jeweller/), attacking a [Gruppe Sechs truck](../gruppe-sechs-trucks/), or buying
from [Lester](../lester/) (civilians only).

## What grows a little every level

- **The pimp's pay**: 1% more per civilian level, up to 50% more.
- **A heads-up race stake**: $1,000 plus $200 per civilian level, up to $6,000. It's always the lower of what the two
  racers' levels and cash allow.
- **Your haggle at the pawn shop**: a 45% chance, plus 2% per Robbing level, up to 80% (from Robbing 18).

## Business reputation

Your Business skill is your reputation as a small-business holder:

| Reputation | Business skill | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 10% off washes and club drinks |
| 2 | 4 | Business jobs cool down 20% faster and pay you 15% more |
| 3 | 7 | Others on your jobs earn 25% more; your jobs earn the business 15% more; a second slot |
| 4 | 11 | Your slots earn 15% more |
| 5 | 16 | Your slots can pay into your paycheck instead of the safe |

wrench at 3; a jerry can at 5.
