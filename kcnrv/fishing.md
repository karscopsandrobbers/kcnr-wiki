No level, team or boat is needed, and the cheapest rod is $180. You can fish without a licence, but the police can
charge you for it. Weights and lengths in game follow your **Measurement System** setting.

## Gear

Two Bait & Tackle shops sell everything: **Crusty Seaman Fishing Tackle** on Del Perro Pier, and **Millar's Fishery
Co.** on the Alamo Sea shore near Grapeseed. Both show on the map. Press <kbd>Y</kbd> on the marker. Everything is
always in stock, and 6% tax goes on top. Gear your Fishing level hasn't reached shows **LEVEL** and the level it needs in
red, and anything you can carry only one of (a rod, a cooler, the licence, a boat upgrade) is bought in one press. You can carry one of each rod, up to 50 of each bait, 5 of each pot, 10 repair
kits, and one of each cooler and the licence.

| Item | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light / Spinning / Heavy Rod | $180 / $650 / $1,800 | 120 / 180 / 240 casts; rated for 4 / 15 / 80 lb (1.8 / 6.8 / 36 kg). The Spinning Rod needs Fishing 4 and the Heavy Rod Fishing 10 |
| Sandworm, Ghost Shrimp | $8 | One bait a cast, up to 50 carried |
| Frozen Mackerel / Cut Squid / Frozen Anchovy or Sardine / Pellets | $7 / $6 / $5 / $4 | All shop baits work the same |
| Crab Pot / Lobster Hoop / Finfish Trap | $550 / $700 / $450 | |
| Rod Repair Kit | $120 | +45 condition on your most worn rod. If no rod is worn, you're told there's nothing to fix and the kit is kept |
| Small / Large Cooler | $4,000 / $10,000 | Carry more fish; the Large needs Fishing 7 |
| Fishing Licence | $350 | No tax at City Hall |

**Rods wear** with every cast: Good, Worn, Poor, Failing, Scrap. A worn rod fails to hook some bites (1 in 10 when
Worn, 1 in 4 when Poor, nearly half when Failing) and snaps lines sooner, and a Scrap rod won't cast.

**Bait:** every cast uses one, bite or not. Small fish you caught yourself (anchovy, sardine, jacksmelt, mackerel,
queenfish, bluegill) go on the hook first and bring more bites and rarer fish. The **smallest** one you carry is used,
and it takes the place of the shop bait for that cast, so the shop bait isn't used up. **A fish of your own is enough
to cast with**: you don't need any shop bait as well.

**Cut bait:** any fish you caught can be cut into bait, from `/fish` (**Cut a fish into bait**, or **Cut all undersize
into bait**) or from your pockets (<kbd>I</kbd>, the fish, **Cut into bait**). Each pound of fish gives 1 to 3 pieces, and a
piece works like shop bait. Cut bait goes on the hook before shop bait. **Bait cut from an undersize or protected fish is
illegal to carry**: an officer who searches you and finds it charges you.

**Fish are real items.** They survive a relog, show in your inventory with their size and weight, and can be handed to
another player in a [trade](../trading/). The pawn counter refuses them, and bait too.

## Where to fish

| Water | Where | What lives there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea | The Pacific and the harbours | Most species, and the most money |
| Fresh | Creeks and rivers, Lago Zancudo, Land Act Reservoir, Mirror Park lake, Mount Gordo | Trout, bass, crappie, bluegill, catfish, carp. Less money, no boat needed, never fishes out completely |
| Brackish | The Alamo Sea and Lake Vinewood | Catfish, carp, striped bass |

**In open water, what bites depends on where your line lands.** Close to shore has the small fish; further out the bass, sheephead, cabezon and halibut; offshore and deep water the yellowtail, white
seabass and lingcod. **Inside one of the hidden spots, the fish are whatever that spot holds**, whatever the distance:
a spot can have species you wouldn't find there in open water.

**Good spots aren't on the map.** Birds circle low over them, and the more birds (2 to 11), the fuller the spot. You
can see them from a long way off, and a nearby gunshot scares them off for a couple of minutes. Only the
sea spots have birds: the creeks, rivers and lakes are spots too, but you won't see gulls over them.

| Where you cast | Chance of a bite | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A full spot | 85% | about 3 s |
| A fished-out spot | about 26% | up to 9 s |
| Water with no spot | about 61% | about 7 s |

Each fish you land, or pull from a pot, takes a little out of a spot's stock, the bait fish
least and the Dungeness crab and lobster most. Spots refill on their own, a sea spot in about an hour and a half
Brackish and fresh water refill faster and never run completely dry.

## Cast, bite, fight

1. **Cast:** face the water and press <kbd>J</kbd>. A "Cast a line" prompt shows on the button bar whenever a cast
   would land in water. You can't cast while swimming, with a gun out, from a car, with no water in front of you, with
   your hands full, without a rod or bait, or with a scrap rod. A full pocket stops the cast too ("Sell what you have
   first"). While you wait for a bite, <kbd>J</kbd> reels the line in; mid-fight it does nothing. Dying, drawing a gun
   or swimming ends the cast, and a wait ends if the boat starts moving, but a fight carries on.
2. **The bite:** when the panel says "IT IS ON", you have **2.6 seconds** to press <kbd>E</kbd>.
3. **The fight:** hold <kbd>E</kbd> to reel. The distance drops but the tension climbs; let go and the tension falls,
   but the fish runs. The bar turns orange at 60% and red at 85%, and at the top the line snaps. Just holding
   <kbd>E</kbd> snaps every line. Big fish fight harder, and a fish heavier than your rod's rating harder still.

**Sizes:** every fish is measured and weighed against the real California minimums. An undersize fish still goes into
your pockets, but nobody buys it: `/fish` and **Put back all undersize** clears them. The orange **garibaldi** is
protected and always goes back, though it still pays the 25 XP and counts as a new species the first time.

**A trophy** is a legal fish that comes close to its species' record weight. Only a legal one earns the trophy
XP and bonus.

## From a boat

Stop below about 5 km/h (3 mph), or drop anchor with <kbd>-</kbd>. The boat has to be in the water. On an open boat
at anchor you can fish from the driver's seat (Dinghy, Jetmax, Seashark, Speeder, Squalo, Suntrap, Toro, Tropic,
Marquis, Longfin); on other boats use a passenger seat or the deck, and a boat with an enclosed helm needs you to move
to an open spot on it. Not from the Avisa, the Submersibles, the Predator, the Patrol Boat or the Tug.

**The anchor** is <kbd>-</kbd> (the numpad minus works too), from the helm or from beside a boat you've just stepped off.
You're told "Anchor down" or "Anchor up". An anchored boat's engine is off, and it stays at anchor while you sit at the
wheel; to turn the engine on you lift the anchor first, and lifting it starts the engine again unless the tank is
empty. Water that's too shallow won't hold it, and you're told the boat can't anchor there. Getting into a boat in the
water shows its keys at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds: the anchor, <kbd>J</kbd> to cast, <kbd>=</kbd> for
a fish finder you've fitted, and <kbd>X</kbd> to set a pot if you carry one.

**Boat upgrades** go on a boat you own, moored within 150 m of the counter:

| Upgrade | Price | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish Finder | $12,000 | A sounder under the hull (<kbd>=</kbd> at the helm) |
| Fish Finder Mk II | $28,000 | Needs the first. A sharper picture, and it reads a fished-out spot |
| Hold Extension / Extension II | $9,000 / $18,000 | +150 lb (68 kg) each, and one more pot in the water; the second needs the first |

Upgrades are taxed 6% like the rest, and fitted to a hull, so you bring the boat to the dock: they aren't sold in a
box. A boat that already has one can't have it twice, and the second of a pair is refused without the first.

**The fish finder** is off until you press <kbd>=</kbd>. It only works at the helm of **your own** boat with a finder
fitted, with the hull in the water. The picture scrolls with the distance you travel (parked, it crawls), and when you're going fast it reads `FAST` and shows no fish. Its readout says HOLDING, CONTACT, BAIT, BOTTOM, BURNT (Mk II
only), FAST or NO DATA. BEARING is your bow, RANGE is how much water the screen covers and DEPTH is under the
hull; depth and range follow your Measurement System setting. The Mk I is a SONAR (wide, noisier, misses more); the Mk II a CHIRP (narrow, sharper, catches nearly everything).

## Pots and traps

Set one from a boat: carry a pot and a shop bait, then press <kbd>X</kbd> in the boat to **Set a pot**. It works any
time you're in a boat, though the on-screen boat bar only lists it briefly after you board. Setting one uses up the
pot and the bait. Three in the water at once, plus one for each Hold Extension on the boat you set it from (so up to
five), each 25 m (82 ft) from anyone else's; you can't set one where another is.
To haul, <kbd>E</kbd> at the buoy, then hold <kbd>E</kbd> on the rope like a fight. Anyone can pull any
pot, but pulling somebody else's is stealing it.

**The pot comes back to your pockets** after a haul, worn, which frees the slot. A pot lasts 25 pulls (the Crab Pot and
Lobster Hoop) or 20 (the Finfish Trap), and a worn pot gives fewer fish. **The rope can part even when you win the fight**, more often on somebody else's pot or a worn one. A parted rope wears the pot faster and can lose it, and stealing wears a pot faster too. Lose the fight and the rope parts too.

| Left for | What you pull up |
| --- | --- |
| Under 5 min | One thing at most |
| 5 to 40 min | A full pot, up to 6 |
| 40 to 90 min | Some of it has gone |
| 90 min to 3 h | Most of it has walked out |
| 3 h or more | Nothing |

In open water a pot only catches nearshore sea species: the Crab Pot rock and yellow rock crab, the Lobster Hoop spiny
lobster, and the Finfish Trap anchovy and sardine. **Dungeness crab, spot prawn and catfish only turn up in particular
spots**, and a pot set inside a spot can only catch what that spot holds. Pot catches drain the spot's stock too.

Each pull comes with a line telling you which band you hit: too soon ("it needed longer"), past its best ("some of it
had gone"), most of it walked out, or empty. Pulling someone else's pot also tells you it's not yours.

## Carrying

Your pockets hold **25 lb (11.3 kg) of fish**. A **Small Cooler** adds 60 lb (27 kg) and a **Large Cooler** 140 lb
(63.5 kg), one of each. **Only a cooler adds room**: a bag doesn't. When you're full the rod won't cast.

A boat's hold carries fish too (120, 300 or 600 lb by hull, plus 150 lb per extension), but buyers only take what's in
your pockets and coolers.

## Selling

At either Bait & Tackle, <kbd>Y</kbd> and **Sell Catch** sells everything you carry. It only shows when you carry a fish
the counter will buy. **`/fish` at the counter** lists
every fish with its price: pick one to sell it on its own, or **Sell the lot**. Pawn shops refuse fish. You're told
what you sold, for how much and how the market stands; undersize fish are left with you ("the buyer will not take
them"), and with nothing to sell you're told so.

Away from a counter, `/fish` (also `/fishes` and `/creel`) shows your catch, and choosing **any** fish puts it back. The
menu shows your Fishing rank and species landed (for example 12/37), and the weight you carry against your limit, what
it's worth and how many are undersize.

A fish's price is its weight times its species' price, plus $200 to $600 if it's a **trophy**, less the counter's cut
(15%, or 5% with a boat you own moored within 150 m). Then it's adjusted for how much that counter has bought lately:
each fish sold lowers its price, to 60% at worst, and it recovers about 2 minutes per fish, up to 120%. The other
counter isn't affected. Each fish sold is also worth 4 civilian XP.

## The licence and the police

The licence is **$350 at City Hall** with no tax, or on the tackle shelf with tax. An officer beside you can check it
if you've cast in the last 5 minutes and carry a working rod: without one it's **Unlicensed Fishing**, one star. A
search that finds bait cut from an undersize or protected fish is **Undersize Bait**, one star too.
Officers can also inspect any pot (<kbd>G</kbd> at the buoy), which shows who owns it, how many minutes it's been
down, how many fish are inside and its condition, and calls it ghost gear past 90 minutes. Or they can seize it
(<kbd>X</kbd>): the pot and its catch are destroyed, and the city pays the officer $150.

## Getting better at it

The **Fishing** skill has 25 levels; `/fish` shows your rank and how many of the 37 species you've landed.

| You | Fishing XP |
| --- | --- |
| Land a legal fish (even the protected garibaldi) | 25 |
| Land an undersize fish | 6 |
| A species you've never landed | +15 |
| A legal trophy | +120 |
| Haul a pot with anything kept | 38 |

Level 4, for the Spinning Rod, is 253 XP: about 11 legal fish. Level 7, for the Large Cooler, is 868 XP: about 35. Level
10, for the Heavy Rod, is 1,902 XP: about 77.

**The work board** has fish jobs too: "Sell five fish" and the weekly "thirty fish" count legal fish sold at a counter,
one per fish, whichever team you're on. Undersize fish and the garibaldi can never be sold, so they don't count. Your
fish caught, trophies, money earned and pots hauled also feed the <kbd>F10</kbd> leaderboards (see [Achievements and leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/)).

## Tips

- Buy the licence at City Hall: no tax.
- Carry only the rod you want to use. You fish with your least-worn working rod, and a repair kit goes to your most
  worn.
- Buy the cheapest bait: Pellets at $4 do what Sandworm at $8 does.
- Ease off at orange, and never sit in the red.
- Put your shorts back, or cut them into bait and use them before an officer finds them. They fill your limit and nobody
  buys them.
- Spread your selling between the two counters.
- Moor your own boat near the counter before selling: you keep 95% instead of 85%.
- Pull pots between 5 and 40 minutes.
- Few birds means a fished-out spot: move on.
