## When you can start

Once the walkthrough is over: its last step is a hold-up. Civilians only, no level needed. You need something in your
hand that frightens the clerk: any gun, melee weapon or thrown weapon, even a stun gun. Bare hands, a petrol can, a
fire extinguisher, a ball, a snowball or a flare do nothing. You can't rob while cuffed, in jail, on a mission or in
another robbery. Before the walkthrough reaches its hold-up step, a robbery is refused.

## Where

The 24/7s and the gas stations with a shop, liquor stores, Ammu-Nations, the pawn shop in Strawberry, the clothing
stores (Ponsonbys, Suburban, Binco, Discount Store), Pibwasser's Country Bar, Bahama Mamas, Tequi-la-la and the Vanilla
Unicorn. [The jeweller](../the-jeweller/) and [the banks](../banks-and-hacking/) have pages of their own.

## How it works

1. **Aim at the clerk.** The robbery starts at once, and a bar shows how frightened he is. Alone, it fills in about 3.3
   seconds with a knife, 2.2 with a pistol and 1.2 with a shotgun. A second robber aiming in the first 3 seconds adds a
   lot; a shot near him jumps the bar; wounding him jumps it further and can kill him.
2. **He gives in at the top of the bar.** He opens the till and hands cash over every second, straight into your
   pocket. He keeps going while the bar stays above 70%, so you can lower the gun to reload.
3. **The panic button.** The moment he gives in he's pressed it: the police are told a robbery is in progress at that
   shop, without your name. Most alarms are silent; Ammu-Nation's screams, and whoever tripped it is told the police know where they are. A loud alarm flashes the shop on every officer's map and rings for up to 2 minutes; people on the street run from it.
4. **The till empties** in 9 to 26 seconds. Each extra robber on the same clerk makes him 35% faster, and a worn
   duffel bag faster too. A shot while he's emptying the till hurries him up for a few seconds. A clerk
   with an empty till stands with his hands up and says so; the first time you hear it, you get a hint on what to do next.
5. **The safe**, if the shop has one:
   - <kbd>E</kbd> works the combination, which the clerk gives whoever held him up, good for 90 seconds. It takes 5
     seconds at the dial and makes no noise. Without it you crack it by ear: two goes a job. Tap the key to hear the
     click; hold it to sweep the dial fast and silently. When you find the click, the dial and number flash green and
     your pad rumbles. Three wrong settings lose that go.
   - <kbd>G</kbd> drills it: you need a drill, and it can overheat. The noise is reported as a "Safe cracking" scene.
   - <kbd>H</kbd> cuts it with a blow torch in 45 seconds, and it's reported too.
   - Opening a safe is robbing the shop: if no robbery is running, it starts one. If the job ends while you're still
     at it, the safe stays shut.
   - Drills and blow torches are on the counter of every 24/7 and gas station; Ammu-Nation always stocks the blow
     torch (and the gas mask).
6. **It ends** a second after the last robber walks out, 10 seconds after there's nothing left, or after 10 minutes.
   Quitting the game mid-job with any of the loot on you still counts as the robbery: you're charged, the cooldowns
   start, and the last robber leaving shuts the shop.

## Crews and the split

Up to **two robbers** a shop: to join, aim at the clerk. In a club every barman has his own till. Till cash is shared on
each handover between the robbers on that clerk; the safe and any wallets are split evenly at the end. Every robber gets
the XP in full.

## Pay

| Shop | One till holds |
| --- | --- |
| 24/7, gas station | $800 to $2,000 |
| Liquor store | $1,000 to $2,500 |
| Bar | $1,200 to $3,000 |
| Clothing store | $1,500 to $3,500 |
| Pawn shop | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| Nightclub, Vanilla Unicorn | $2,000 to $4,500 a bar |
| Ammu-Nation | $2,000 to $5,000 |
| A shop safe | $900 to $3,200, then empty for 6 hours |

Each robbery you get away with is **60 civilian XP and 20 Robbing XP**.

## Armed clerks

Some clerks keep a gun under the counter and shoot instead of opening the till:

| Shop | Chance | Weapon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ammu-Nation | 50% | Pump shotgun |
| Pawn shop | 30% | Pistol |
| Liquor store | 15% | Pistol |
| Everywhere else | 5% | Pistol |

Shoot him in the forearm or hand to disarm him and he goes back to the till; anywhere else can kill him. If he dies
before he opened the till, the job pays nothing; after, you keep what he handed over.

## The customers

The customers nearby are caught up in it. Each cowers, kneels, hides, runs, phones the police, or has a
go at you. Panic spreads from the ones who run or cower to the people near them. Point a gun at someone dialling and
the phone drops. Hold a gun on someone kneeling, cowering or hiding, up close for a moment, and they
hand over their wallet ($20 to $400), and will certainly phone it in afterwards.

A customer on the phone shows as a red dot on the map to robbers and wanted players, with a red marker over their head
when you're near, and "On the phone to the police" with a countdown once you're close.

What they do depends on the building: at the jeweller most cower, at Ammu-Nation most run, and everywhere else it's a mix.

A gun in your hand makes the ones who would have a go rarer. Point it at one who does and he usually backs down.

## Afterwards

- A hold-up anybody noticed is **1 star**, and 1 more if somebody reports it. The panic button means an ordinary shop
  always notices. A statement from somebody who only saw a mask gives the police a description of the mask, not a name. See [Wanted level and heat](../wanted-level-and-heat/).
- The shop shuts for **10 minutes**, and you can't rob it again for 10 minutes; every other shop is fair game. `/rcd`
  lists your cooldowns. Walk in meanwhile and a wanted customer is turned away ("I can't help you if the cops are after
  you!"); anybody else is told how many minutes until it reopens, or, after a scare that never became a robbery, that
  there was a situation here.
- **Hot cash.** While an officer is online and you're still inside that job's red circle, being arrested takes the
  till money off you, and dying drops it. Out of the circle, it's just money.
- **Shops remember.** One hold-up that paid, or a gun on the staff twice, and they know your face (or the mask you
  wore) next time. Go back soon after, or a third time, and
  the clerk goes for the alarm the moment you walk in: a short warning, then a few seconds depending on how
  steady he is, with a ring over his head and an ALARM bar. Walk out, hold a gun on him until his hands go up, or hurt or
  gas him, and it stops. A look the staff didn't see teaches them nothing. The grudge fades with time, and
  buying something there buys a little back.

## Tips

- Mask up before you go in: a camera that sees your bare face names you on the spot.
- Bring a second robber: the till opens faster and empties faster.
- A drill or blow torch counts as illegal in a police search.
