Anyone, on any team. Both of you must be on foot, alive, not cuffed, not being arrested or searched, and not in jail.

## Handing something over

<kbd>I</kbd>, pick a pile, **Give to…**, pick somebody within 3.5 m, then the amount. It's instant and one-way: they
can't refuse it, and nothing comes back.

## The trade table

1. Look at the player, press <kbd>X</kbd> and pick **Trade** (within 2.5 m).
2. They answer <kbd>Y</kbd> to accept or <kbd>L</kbd> to decline. **They have 10 seconds**, and no answer is a no.
3. You each get a table:
   - **Put something up:** it leaves your pockets and sits on your side.
   - **Cash:** put up $10 to $100,000, or everything you carry.
   - **Agree to this:** once you've both agreed, the swap happens. Any change by either of you clears both
     agreements.
   - **Call it off:** everything goes back.

At the swap the cash is checked again. If somebody no longer has it, both agreements clear and you start over. If your
pockets can't take a pile, it goes back to whoever offered it, and the rest of the swap still happens.

**A trade ends**, everything going home, if you're more than 4 m apart, get into a vehicle, die, go to jail, get
cuffed, arrested or searched, teleport, disconnect or close the menu. That holds even across a restart.

**What can't be traded:** anything worn or held in your hands (take it off or put it down first), anything bound to
you, and houses and vehicles.

There's no fee, tax or cooldown.

## Tips

- Use the table for anything valuable. **Give to** is a gift.
- Agree last.
