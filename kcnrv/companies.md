The city's firms run their business whether you are watching or not: a crew on the floor in working hours, stock on
the shelves, a guard on the yard. Each one's depot is a faded **C** on the map, and what is on its shelves can be
carried out, a piece at a time, into a vehicle you brought. **Only what you load into a vehicle counts as your take.**

## Bugstars

Bugstars, the pest control firm, keeps its depot on the Port of Los Santos. Its crew is on the floor from **05:00** to
**21:00** on the game clock, a foreman with his clipboard and the men at the front, and a security guard walks the yard
round the clock.

### Getting in

The depot's four roller doors stay down. One goes up for a **Bugstars van** driven up to it by someone in the Bugstars
overalls, at any hour, and stays up while anybody is in the depot; a van stolen from the depot doesn't open them. On
foot, the **keypad** by the east doors: <kbd>E</kbd> to hack it, one person at a time, and beaten, its door is up for
60 seconds. Fail it, or walk away from it, while the depot is shut or has no crew on the floor, and the alarm goes off.
It's GTA Online's Casino keypad hack, four patterns to repeat, and [Lester's](../lester/) gear and your Hacking skill
each take patterns off, as they ease any hack; a laptop with a USB drive in it covers a wrong code that would have set
the alarm off. See [Hacking](../hacking/).
From inside, a door goes up for anybody who walks or drives up to it. Sound your horn at the doors without the overalls and the guard, then one of the crew, will tell you to clear off. Bugstars vans aren't sold at the dealership.

Three Bugstars vans stand inside. Load one and drive it out, and the doors go up for you, but taking a van is **Grand
Theft Auto**, 2 stars, if the crew sees it, and sets the alarm off when there is no crew to see it. A van taken is
replaced later, once it is empty and nobody is around it.

### What there is to take

| Where | What you carry out | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The barrels | A **drum of acetone** | Break it open (a crowbar, a wrench, a hammer, a bat or a blade) and it is 50 acetone for a [lab](../drugs/) |
| The box piles | A box of **pest control equipment** | The [pawn shop](../selling-stolen-goods/) |
| The gas bottles | A **fumigant canister** | The pawn shop |

Now and then a canister or a box is left lying in the open corners on the east side as well. A drum knocked off its
spot can still be taken where it ended up, as long as it hasn't rolled more than 3 metres.

<kbd>E</kbd> over a pile takes one. A drum or a box takes both arms: you walk, and you can't run or get into a car with
it. Load it into a vehicle's boot with <kbd>E</kbd> at the boot (see [Carrying things](../carrying-things/)), so bring
something with room: a boot holds by weight, and a van's takes far more than a car's (two drums and eight boxes fill
one).

Your first take starts **your own robbery**: bars on screen show how long it has run, what you have loaded and what it
is worth. It is done, for you alone, once you are 40 metres from the depot's doors with something loaded, and only
then does it count. Go down or get cuffed before that and it is over for you, with nothing to show for it. Take
something back out of the boot and it comes off your take until you load it again.

A pile you empty is gone until Bugstars restocks it, which it does slowly, and only while nobody is around.

### The staff and the alarm

The crew leave you alone until something sets them off: a weapon out where they can see it, a gun on one of them,
shots, a body, or stock taken in front of them. Then one or more of them square up with their fists and the rest run,
the alarm goes off and the police are called. The guard joins in.

- Taking stock in front of them is **Robbery**, 2 stars.
- A weapon out in front of them is **Brandishing**, 1 star; hitting or shooting at one of them, 1 star too.

The police see for themselves as well. An officer within 30 m of you sees a take or a van driven off, overalls or not,
and an officer who spots you mid-robbery catches you in the act: **Robbery**, 2 stars, and you can be pursued and
arrested on the spot. Logging off in the middle of a robbery with something taken is still the robbery.

After a while with nobody around, a fresh crew is on the floor.

### After hours

From 21:00 to 05:00 the crew has gone home and the alarm is set: anything taken trips it, and the police are called
to a burglary. It is set the same way in working hours when there is no crew left on the floor. Two ways round it:

- **Cut the power** at the electrical box inside, in the south-east corner, with a blade: no alarm and no lights for 3
  minutes. The police are told somebody tampered with it.
- **Gas it from the roof.** A BZ gas grenade on the big air-conditioning unit puts everybody inside down for 45
  seconds (tear gas, 15), you too without a gas mask.

### Call-outs

While the depot is open, Bugstars sends its vans out on jobs. Every 12 to 25 minutes one of the vans leaves with a crew
of four exterminators, and no more than two are out at once. It goes to a house in **Mirror Park**, **Pacific Bluffs**
or **Richman**, or, one call in two, to a house in the city's own listings, a player's included, where the crew set up
round the front door. They work there 8 to 15 minutes, then climb back in and drive home to the depot.

A van out on a call is easier pickings than the depot:

- **Its boot** holds 3 boxes of pest control equipment and 2 fumigant canisters, the firm's stock like what is on the
  shelves, and taking it starts your robbery the same way. Taken where the crew can see you (within 30 m), it is
  **Robbery**, 2 stars; out of their sight, nobody knows.
- **The van** can be driven off, with whatever is still in its boot counting as your take. If the crew see it go, it
  is **Grand Theft Auto**, 2 stars, and the police are told the van, its plate and where it was taken. A stolen
  Bugstars van is flagged to every police plate reader until it is back at the depot, and a reader that catches you at
  its wheel makes you wanted for it.
- **The crew** are unarmed. A weapon out in front of them, a gun on one of them, shots or a hit sets them off, 1 star:
  the first of them squares up with his fists, the rest fight or run, and the call is over.

In the Bugstars overalls the crew take you for one of their own here too. Putting the firm's stock back into one of its
own vans is giving it back, not loading a take: only your own vehicle counts.

### Afterwards

Take drums to a gang's **stash house** and sell them to the man at the door, <kbd>E</kbd>: everything in your arms and in
the boot of the vehicle you came in goes, as far as the gang's cash stretches. A gang that hasn't had drums for a while
pays the most, and less with each one you sell. Corners don't buy drums, and selling them doesn't raise your standing
with a gang. Walk up with empty hands and the guards leave you be; a gun out, or a gang that hates you, is another
matter. Ring ahead and they expect you, even if they don't like you, and the door goes on your map:

Or break a drum open for your own [lab](../drugs/). A drum is contraband: a frisk or a police dog turns it up. The equipment
and canisters are hot for an hour, like any everyday stolen goods: see [Selling stolen goods](../selling-stolen-goods/).

A stolen Bugstars van is called in by its plate when the crew saw it go or the alarm went, and every police plate
reader knows it until it is back at the depot.

**Officers:** drive a stolen Bugstars van back into the depot and everything of theirs in its boot goes back on the
shelves. Stock carried by hand goes back with "Return stolen stock" at the east doors. Stock that was stolen earns you
good cop credit and XP, up to a limit each hour; an empty van earns nothing.

### The Bugstars overalls

Rob Bugstars **15 times** and take **$50,000** of their stock over those robberies (what you load into a vehicle, at
market price) and you earn their overalls. Only one robbery each half hour counts toward the 15. They show under
**Outfits** at any clothing store or your house's wardrobe, with how far you are until then, and once earned they go on
for nothing. In them the crew take you for one of their own: they don't stop you carrying stock out or driving off in a
van. A weapon out still sets them off. No clothing store sells them.
