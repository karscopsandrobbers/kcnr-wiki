Kar's Cops and Robbers is **soft roleplay with arcade edges**: a city run the way roleplay servers run theirs, with jobs,
property, police and crime, but without strict roleplay rules to learn first. You can work, rob, police, race or fish,
and switch between them.

## Joining

Open FiveM and connect to **cfx.re/join/gaezxq**. The first screen is the login: register an account there, or log in
to the one you have.

**Your account name is your FiveM name.** The server reads it as you connect and decides whether you get a login or a
registration card: a name it knows gets the login, a new one gets registration. To play under another name, change your
FiveM name and connect again. The login card also shows the latest release notes, so you can see what changed since
you were last here.

You are turned away before anything downloads if:

- your FiveM name is empty or longer than 32 characters;
- the account is disabled;
- somebody is already connected under that name;
- you are banned (the reason is shown to you);
- too many players are already connected from your address. Staff set that limit, and it can be off.

### Registering

On a new name the card asks for a password and, if you like, an email:

| | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Password | 5 to 32 characters, typed twice |
| Email | Optional, up to 64 characters. Without one, you can't get a recovery code by email |

Staff can limit how many accounts one address, or one Rockstar licence, may register. If you are over it you are told
how many already exist and asked to log in with one of them.

### Recovering an account

Choose **Forgot your Password?** on the login card:

1. A six-digit code is emailed to the address on the account. The card shows the address with most of it hidden, such
   as `ab****@host`, so you can tell which inbox to open.
2. Type the code and a new password (5 characters or more, typed twice). **The code is good for an hour.** Asking again
   within five minutes sends nothing new, and the code you have still works; after that a new code replaces it.
3. If the account has no email, the card tells you to contact an admin through the website.

Add an email when you register: it is what recovery depends on.

### Your character

Each account has **one character**. You make it in the character creator, which is GTA Online's own: the same pages,
names, cameras and colour swatches, with a few more choices on top. Nothing is saved until you save, and you can't
leave without saving.

**The creator is a one-time visit.** You can't reopen it, so the character you build is final unless staff open it for
you again. What you can change later is what you wear, at a clothing store, and your hair, at a [barber](../barbers/).

### The character creator

| Page | What you choose |
| --- | --- |
| Sex | Male or female. Switching keeps each body's look while you're in the creator, so you can go back and forth without losing either |
| Heritage | Your mother (22 to choose from) and father (24), by GTA Online's names, with both their portraits above the list; **Resemblance** (which parent your face takes after) and **Skin Tone** (whose skin), 0 to 100% |
| Features | Twelve parts of the face. Eight are a grid that moves two ways at once (the nose: narrow to wide, and up to down); the eyes, cheeks, lips and neck are a single bar |
| Appearance | Eyebrows, facial hair, blemishes, ageing, complexion, moles and freckles, sun damage, makeup, blush, lipstick and chest hair, each with how strong it is. One that takes a colour has its colour row right under it, drawn as swatches. Every row is there for both sexes |
| Hair & Colors | Any haircut the barber sells, its colour and highlight (64 each, as swatches), and your eye colour (32) |
| Apparel | A **style** (Street, Flashy, Party, Beach, Smart, Sporty, Eccentric or Casual), one of its **8 outfits**, and a hat and glasses if you want them |

Every page ends with **Randomize** and **Reset** for that page. On the main menu, **Angle** turns your character round,
and for a new character the last row, **Reset to default**, starts the whole character over.

| Key | In the creator |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Randomise the row you're on. On the main menu it randomises the whole character: parents, face, appearance, hair, a natural eye colour and an outfit, with a hat or glasses now and then |
| <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd> (held) | Turn your face to show its profile, left or right. Holding the left mouse button to one side of your character does the same |
| <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> (held) | Turn your character round, inside any page |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> | On a Features grid: across, and up and down. Hold <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> to move three times as fast, or drag inside the grid with the mouse |

**You always leave dressed:** a top, trousers or a skirt, and shoes, at the least. A new character starts in one of
the Apparel page's outfits, picked at random, and can change it before saving; two of the man's Beach outfits are
shirtless, and the creator won't save in those. If staff open the creator for a character you already have, the first choice on each Apparel
row is **current**, the clothes you already wear, and **Cancel** leaves without changing anything.

**Saving asks you to confirm first.** Then the camera turns to the booking board with your name on it and takes your
mugshot, and you're out.

## Where you start

A new character lands on the **arrivals deck at Los Santos International Airport**, facing the exit. After that you
come back where you logged out, or in your house once you own one and set it as your spawn.

**Everybody starts as a civilian, with nothing**: no money, no car, no job. There is no team picker. Police, medic
and firefighter are jobs you sign up for later, at a desk in a police station, hospital or fire station.

For your first 15 minutes nobody can hurt you, and you can't shoot or rob either. The walkthrough turns that
protection off when it reaches its last step. Every time you spawn you also get 10 seconds of protection, which ends
early if you draw a weapon.

## If you die

**The whole server is told.** Everybody sees a chat line saying who died, who killed them, and with what: the weapon's
name, a gang's name beside it when one of its members did it, "run over by" a car, "crushed", a police officer, a civilian,
an animal, or **misadventure** for a fall or a drowning. You see a WASTED banner with the cause. Every kill and death
counts toward the [leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/). If you were cuffed, the cuffs come off.

You come back at the nearest hospital after 8 seconds (3 during a Purge). Everything that happens, in order, is on [Death and respawn](../death-and-respawn/). If you died right beside that hospital,
you come back at the next nearest one instead, so you never wake up beside whoever killed you. You appear on the hospital
grounds, and a notice says you were discharged from it by name.

The hospital bill is 2% of the cash you are carrying, at least $50 and at most $1,000, and never more than you have. Chat
shows the hospital's name and the amount, in green. See [Chat and Discord](../chat-and-discord/) for the rest of chat.

There is no revive. If you were in jail, you go back to your cell.
