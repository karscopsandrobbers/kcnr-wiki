Every character has a phone and a number of their own, **555** and four digits, given the first time you play and
kept for good. It isn't an item: nobody can take it, and you can't lose it.

Press <kbd>Up</kbd> to take it out, and <kbd>Up</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to put it away. You can still walk while it's
out; while you type, the keys are the phone's. Everybody near you sees it in your hand. You can't use it in cuffs, in a
cell, or while you're down.

## Calls

Open **Phone** and dial a number on the keypad, or call from **Recents** or **Contacts**. Their phone rings out loud,
so people near them hear it too, and they answer on the card that comes up (<kbd>Y</kbd>) or on their phone.

Once they answer you hear each other over voice chat however far apart you are, through a phone's tinny line.
Push-to-talk works as it always does. Either of you can hang up. A call nobody answers is a **missed call** on their
phone, and you're told if you have any when you log in.

A job's contact ringing you and a call between players are the same line: on one, you can't be rung for the other.

**911** on the keypad goes to the police: say what is happening, and they're told where you are.

## Messages

Open **Messages** to text a number or a contact, up to 160 characters, with emoji. You can also send:

- **A photo** from your Photos (the camera button in a conversation).
- **A pin on the map** where you're standing (the pin button). Whoever gets it taps it to set a waypoint there.

A text that comes in while your phone is away shows on screen with a tone. Texts wait for somebody who isn't on.

## Contacts

Save a number with a name in **Contacts**, and it shows by name in your calls and messages. From a contact you can text,
call, or **Block** them: a blocked number can't ring or text you, and they aren't told.

## Camera and Photos

**Camera** takes real photos: <kbd>Space</kbd> or the left mouse button takes one, <kbd>E</kbd> turns the camera
round for a selfie, the scroll wheel zooms, and <kbd>Left Ctrl</kbd> or the right mouse button puts it away. Your photos
are in **Photos**: look at one, send it in a message, or delete it. Your phone keeps your 40 newest.

## Settings

Your own number, a choice of ringtones and wallpapers, and **Silent**: no ringtone and no text tone, though your
phone still shows what came.
