Every character has a phone and a number of their own, like **555-0142**, given the first time you play and kept for
good. It isn't an item: nobody can take it, and you can't lose it. Numbers start on 555; once those are all given out,
new players get 556, then 557, and so on. To ring a 555 number you can dial just its last four digits.

Press <kbd>Up</kbd> to take it out, and <kbd>Up</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to put it away. You can still walk while it's
out; while you type, the keys are the phone's. Scroll with the mouse wheel, or click and drag a list. Everybody near
you sees it in your hand. You can't use it in cuffs, in a cell, or while you're down.

## Calls

Open **Phone** and dial a number on the keypad (clicking it or typing the digits, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to call), or call
from **Recents** or **Contacts**. Their phone rings out loud,
so people near them hear it too, and they answer on the card that comes up (<kbd>Y</kbd>) or on their phone.

Put your phone away during a call and it stays peeking up from the bottom of the screen, with who you're talking to and
for how long; take it out again to hang up.

Once they answer you hear each other over voice chat however far apart you are, through a phone's tinny line.
Push-to-talk works as it always does. Either of you can hang up. A call nobody answers is a **missed call** on their
phone, and you're told if you have any when you log in.

A job's contact ringing you and a call between players are the same line: on one, you can't be rung for the other. A
contact who rings while you're talking to a player waits, and rings once you hang up.

**911** on the keypad goes to the police: say what is happening, and they're told where you are.

## Messages

Open **Messages** to text a number or a contact, up to 160 characters, with emoji. You can also send:

- **A photo** from your Photos (the camera button in a conversation).
- **A pin on the map** where you're standing (the pin button). Whoever gets it taps it to set a waypoint there.

A text that comes in shows at the top of your phone with a tone; if your phone is away, it peeks up from the bottom
of the screen to show it and goes back down. Click one with your phone out to open the conversation. Texts wait for somebody who isn't on. A
text from **KCNR Staff** is from the server's admins; you can't reply to it, so answer with `/report` if you need to.

## Contacts

Save a number with a name in **Contacts** (the **+** at the top, also on the Phone's Contacts tab), or type it on the
keypad and press **Add to contacts**, and it shows by name in your calls and messages. From a contact you can text,
call, or **Block** them: a blocked number can't ring or text you, and they aren't told.

## Camera and Photos

**Camera** takes real photos, with its keys on screen: <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> or the left mouse button
takes one, the middle mouse button turns the camera round for a selfie (rebind it in FiveM's key bindings), the scroll wheel zooms,
and <kbd>Backspace</kbd>, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the right mouse button puts it away and brings your phone back. The rest are
GTA's own phone camera keys, so they follow whatever you've bound them to in its settings. Your photos
are in **Photos**: look at one, send it in a message, or delete it. Your phone keeps your 40 newest.

## Settings

Your own number (it's also on your <kbd>F10</kbd> profile and at the top of Contacts), a choice of ringtones and
wallpapers, and **Silent**: no ringtone and no text tone, though your
phone still shows what came.
