Your total level goes to 100 and each skill to 25. Past level 10 there's nothing left to unlock but faster cars and
bigger jobs.

## At total level 10: Premium Deluxe Motorsport

It sells sports cars, supercars and convertibles. Prices follow how fast a car is: from $3,000 for an Asea to
$2,500,000 for an Emerus, never more than $5,000,000. A motorbike costs a tenth of a car as fast, and Sanders sells
them at any level. At the mod shop, the engine, brakes and turbo are what make a getaway. See
[Vehicles](../vehicles/).

## The top of the pimp's ladder

| Job | Civilian level | Base pay | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Hold the turf* | 11 | $3,600 | 160 |
| *VIP Escort* | 15 | $6,000 | 220 |

A VIP client tips $2,400 to $5,600 when you put down the men who came for him, and sometimes $800 to $2,200 on a
quiet night. By civilian level 15 the pay is 15% higher, rising 1% a level to 50%. See [The pimp](../the-pimp/).

## The top of tow dispatch

- **Towing 10**: insurance recoveries, $4,000 base and 130 XP.
- **Towing 15**: the scrapyard texts you an offer for the car on your hook, two to four times the fee. Taking it is a
  crime.

## Hacking 10 and 20

Each makes every bank hack one step easier, and the password terminal at Pacific Standard gets easier with every
level. See [Banks and hacking](../banks-and-hacking/).

## The big jobs

None of these needs more than total level 3, but they're where a crew with gear and skill pays off:

- **[Vangelico](../the-jeweller/)**: 300 XP and 60 Robbing. It pays in pieces you carry in a duffel bag on your back
  and sell at a pawn shop.
- **Pacific Standard** (`/heists`): $30,000 to $60,000 a trolley.
- **[Gruppe Sechs trucks](../gruppe-sechs-trucks/)**: briefcases of $5,000 to $10,000, bags of $12,000 to $22,000, a
  bigger bank's bag $40,000 to $70,000.

At **total level 15** you can also buy from Vangelico's cases.

## Your business, later

Reputation 4 (Business 11) makes your slots earn 15% more, and reputation 5 (Business 16) lets them pay into your
paycheck. See [Businesses](../businesses/).

## The long game

- The weekly board pays $8,000 to $10,000.
- The leaderboards run a **This Week** board that resets every Sunday (UTC), so a new player can top it.
- 24 hours of play makes you a regular, announced to everyone.
