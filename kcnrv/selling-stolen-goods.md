## Pawn shops

Press <kbd>Y</kbd> at a pawn shop's counter: opening the menu tips out of your bag everything the shop would buy, and it buys what you are holding in your hands as well as what is in your pockets (see [Carrying things](../carrying-things/)). Guns, grenades and the like stay in the bag. A pawn shop never buys a bag itself, only what's in it, and it skips the
phone the walkthrough gives you. The menu lists what the shop pays most for first, so your valuables are at the top.

Park the vehicle you came in outside and the shop buys out of its boot too: what's in it is on the counter with the
rest, so a van full of boxes doesn't have to be carried in one at a time. It has to be the one you last drove, or your
own: not just any car parked at the door.

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Valuables (jewellery, watches, gold bars and so on) | 60% of market price |
| Ordinary second-hand goods | 25% |
| Contraband, bags, fish, bait, sealed G6 cargo, anything with no market price | Not bought |

**Hot pieces.** Anything stolen is hot for a while after it is taken: jewellery from a case or a deposit box, gold bars
out of a G6 bag, a firm's stock, whatever you take in a stick-up or lift from a pocket, and anything a customer saw you
shoplift. Your inventory marks it
**Stolen** and keeps it in a pile of its own, apart from any of the same thing you came by honestly. The heat is on the
goods, not on you, so it goes wherever they go: hand them to a friend, drop them, put them in a boot or log off, and they
are still hot for whoever has them. Once it wears off they are ordinary goods again and join the rest of the pile.

How long depends on how easily the piece is recognised:

| Stolen | Hot for |
| --- | --- |
| Everyday things: a phone, a wallet, sunglasses, a silver chain, a steel or digital watch, a gold ring, a laptop or TV, a firm's stock | 1 hour |
| Fine things: gold, diamonds and the other stones, pearls, the gold, luxury and platinum watches, coins and bonds, collector's pieces, art and trophies | 4 hours |
| One of a kind: the rare painting, the rare trophy | For good. No pawn shop will buy it |

A hot piece sells for 55% of the usual offer, and when you sell one, about one time in three the shop tips off the
police, with your name if your face was showing. Only the stolen pieces are marked down: sell four of something with
one off a job, and the other three fetch the full price. An officer who finds hot pieces on you in a frisk can book them
into evidence, and an arrest mid-robbery takes a firm's stolen stock straight off you.

**Haggling.** One try per item: your chance is 45%, plus 2% per Robbing level, up to 80%. Win and the price goes up 10
to 25%; lose and he drops his price 15%, or now and then refuses that item for the visit.

A pawn shop that's been robbed is shut and buys nothing until it reopens.

## The G6 insider

A fence who snitches on Gruppe Sechs: he tips you off about their runs and buys what you take off their trucks, no
questions asked. You'll find him out by Grapeseed; his faded marker shows only when you're near. Press <kbd>Y</kbd> on
him (civilians only; anyone in a uniform is told to walk on). His menu opens when you're next to him.

- **He buys all your sealed G6 cargo at once**, from your pockets and your bag: its full worth for a clean piece, 85%
  for one with a dye pack, and 10 Robbing XP a piece, with a free tip on the next truck if he knows one. He buys cargo you're holding in your hands
too.
- **A tip costs $1,500**: a 60 m circle near the next truck's stop, with roughly how long until it arrives. It goes
  once the truck has been and gone, the run is lost, or 10 minutes after it was due. He only gives a tip for a truck you aren't robbing and don't already have a tip on, and
  you can hold at most five tips at once.

## What to sell where

| You have | Best place | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jewellery from a case or a deposit box | A pawn shop | Wait out the heat if you can (an hour, or four for the fine pieces): full price, no tip-off |
| A sealed cash box, bag or duffel | The G6 insider | Full worth, never less than opening it yourself, plus Robbing XP |
| A sealed gold bag | The G6 insider | Its full worth; opened, each bar fetches only 60% at a pawn shop |
| Till or vault cash | Nothing to sell | It's already money |
