## Three kinds of level

**Your total level** is your whole character, the rank bar you see when you press <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd>. It's
what opens the big things: banks, groups, leasing a business, the premium dealership. It goes to 100. It grows from:

- **all of your team XP**: every point you earn on a team also counts here;
- **a few awards of its own**: the walkthrough (150), your first goals, a lottery win (150). Of all the
  [achievements](../achievements-and-leaderboards/), only your first goals pay anything.

Skill XP never counts toward your total.

**Team ranks.** Civilian, Law Enforcement, Medic and Firefighter each have their own rank, up to 100. Switching teams
loses nothing; the old rank waits for you. **Team XP only counts for the team you're on right now**: sell fish or
finish a board job as a medic and you get the cash, but no civilian XP and nothing toward your total.

- **Civilian rank** opens the pimp's harder jobs and raises his pay, and sets how big a heads-up race stake can be.
- **Police rank** opens the armoury's weapons and keeps your roadblocks and stingers down longer.
- **Firefighter rank** opens the station's equipment.

**Skills** go to 25, and each grows from its own work:

| Skill | Grows from | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hacking | successful alarm panel and vault terminal hacks | Level 10 makes every hack a step easier, level 20 another |
| Robbing | robberies you get away with, and sealed G6 cargo sold to the insider | A better haggle at the pawn shop |
| Towing | tow calls delivered | It is your tow rank: better calls and trucks |
| Fishing | every fish landed and every pot pulled | Fishing 7 lets you buy the large cooler |
| Business | business jobs and collecting a safe | Your small-business reputation: discounts, a second slot, bigger earnings |
| Mechanics | nothing yet | Shown on your skills page, but nothing raises it today |

## You'll know when it happens

Every time you earn XP, GTA's rank bar flashes. A level-up is announced to the whole server, with confetti for a team
or total level. To check where you stand:

- <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd>: your total level;
- `/level`: your team rank, and `/total`: your total level;
- **Skills & ranks** in the <kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard: everything, with your tow rank under Towing;
- **Profile** in the dashboard: a card for each team with your counts on it, and the
  [leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/) to see how you stack up.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Finish the walkthrough | 150 | Total |

| Win the lottery | 150 | Total |

| Crush a car at the scrapyard | 15 | Your team |
| A successful hack | 40 | Civilian |
| | 46 | Hacking |
| A fish | 25 a keeper, 6 an undersize one, +15 for a new species, +120 for a trophy | Fishing |
| A pot pulled | 38 | Fishing |
| Selling fish | 4 a fish | Civilian |
| Sealed G6 cargo sold to the insider | 10 a piece | Robbing |
| Racing | 40 to finish, +120/70/40 on the podium, 50 for a personal best, 200 for a server record | Civilian |
| Today's work | 100 to 200 a daily, 800 to 1,000 the weekly | Civilian |
| A business job / collecting a safe | 40 / 10 | Business |
| A police arrest | 40 + 10 a star, plus bonuses; a takedown pays three quarters | Police |

| A fire | 6 a flame, +60 for everyone there when it's out | Firefighter |

The team curve is gentler than the total's, so your civilian rank usually runs a little ahead of your total level.
