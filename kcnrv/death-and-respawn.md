Dying in KCNR is cheap on purpose. There is no revive, no lost levels and no loot lying on your body. You lose a small
bill, whatever was in your hands (see [Carrying things](../carrying-things/)), and sometimes a worn bag. This page is everything that happens, in order.

## When you die

- **Everybody is told in chat** who killed whom, and with what: "died (Fists, by a Ballas member)", "killed *name*",
  "committed suicide", or just "died". A car is "run over by" its name, a heavy object is "crushed", and a fall, a
  drowning or the map itself is "misadventure". A police officer, an animal (see [Wildlife](../wildlife/)) or a gang member is named too.
- **You see a WASTED shard** under the same cause.
- **Kills and deaths are counted** on the [leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/), a kill for the one who got it and a
  death for you.
- **Your cuffs come off**, and anything in your hands is put down where you fell.
- **Whatever you were doing ends.** A job or mission you were on fails with no pay (see each job's page), a trade
  is called off with everything going home, an open boot shuts, a dumpster search or a stick-up in progress stops, and a robbery
  you were part of ends for you.

## Waking up

You get a respawn button with a countdown on screen. It runs **8 seconds**, or **3 seconds during
[the Purge](../the-purge/)**. Then:

| Where you were | Where you wake |
| --- | --- |
| Anywhere in the world | The hospital **nearest to where you fell**, at a spot in its grounds |
| Right beside that hospital | The **next nearest** hospital, so you never wake up beside whoever killed you |
| In a cell, serving a sentence | Back in a cell, with no hospital bill. Dying is not a way out of jail |
| Killed by an officer at 4 or more stars | In a cell, as if you'd been arrested: see [Jail and the law](../jail-and-the-law/) |
| Inside a house or a mod shop | At a hospital in the open world, like anybody else. Your **Spawn in house** setting only applies when you log in |

Your team makes no difference: a civilian, a police officer, a medic and a firefighter all wake the same way. You're
told "Discharged from *hospital*" when you arrive.

## The hospital bill

Every death that doesn't end in a cell costs you **2% of the cash you're carrying, at least $50 and at most $1,000,
and never more than you have**. It comes out of your cash only, never your bank, and chat shows the amount in green.

| Cash on you | The bill |
| --- | --- |
| $0 to $2,500 | $50 (or all you have, if less) |
| $5,000 | $100 |
| $10,000 | $200 |
| $50,000 and up | $1,000 |

Alive and hurt, a hospital's desk heals you to full for the same bill: see [Medic](../medic/).

The bill is the same in a Purge. It goes to the city's treasury: see [Money](../money/).

## What you lose

| You lose | When |
| --- | --- |
| The hospital bill above | Every death, unless you wake in a cell |
| Whatever you were holding in your hands, put down where you fell | Every death |
| A **worn bag and everything in it**, dropped where you fell for anybody to pick up | Only if you die wanted, or inside your own crime scene, **and an officer is online**. Otherwise it stays on your back |
| **Till cash** from a hold-up you haven't got away from | Killed at the scene of the robbery with an officer online. It falls where you fell, for anyone to take, not the city |
| Your share of a job's pay | The job fails when you die |

Get clear of the scene and the take is yours, however you die afterwards. With no officer online nothing is lost
but the bill.

## What you keep

- **Your bank**, which nobody can take from you.
- **The items in your pockets.** A death never empties them.
- **Your weapons**, which are given back as you wake.
- **Your cars and your houses.**
- **Your levels and XP.**

## Spawn protection

Every time you spawn you get **10 seconds** in which nobody can hurt you. It ends
the moment you draw a weapon, and you're told when it's over. The first 15 minutes of a new character, the
walkthrough, have their own protection and cover this one.

## Killing other players

A civilian who kills another player commits Murder: 4 stars, or 5 if an officer can see it. See
[Wanted level and heat](../wanted-level-and-heat/). A police officer who kills a civilian with 4 or more stars makes a
takedown, which counts as an arrest and pays three quarters of the arrest XP.

## Tips

- **Bank your cash before a fight.** The bill, a stick-up and a robbery drop all take cash, never your bank.
- **Wear a bag only when you must.** A worn bag is the one thing a death can take, and only while you're wanted.
