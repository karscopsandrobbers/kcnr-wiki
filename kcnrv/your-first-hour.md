## The walkthrough

It starts the moment you register, and it's six short steps. Each one puts a marker or a waypoint on the thing to do:

1. **Welcome.** Chat says to follow the prompts, or leave with `/skipwalkthrough`.
2. **Steal the bag on the curb** (<kbd>E</kbd>). A marker shows the bag. Inside: $250, a pistol with no rounds, a knife or
   a dagger, and a candy bar.
3. **Open your inventory** (<kbd>I</kbd>). The candy bar heals you; the weapons are in your weapon wheel.
4. **Take a car.** A car with nobody in it nearby is marked, and a waypoint points to it. It is never your
   personal vehicle, a police or emergency car, or a job's car. Any car you find yourself counts just the same.
5. **Buy something at a shop** (<kbd>Y</kbd> at the counter). The nearest 24/7 or gas station selling something you can
   afford is marked. If none has anything at your price, one is stocked for you.
6. **Hold the shop up.** The nearest open 24/7 or gas-station store with a clerk is marked: not one you own, and not one
   you robbed lately. Point your weapon at the clerk. The knife or dagger is enough.

For your first 15 minutes nobody can hurt you and you can't hurt anybody. **The hold-up step turns that off**, and you
are told so: "Passive mode is off. This one is real: the police can come for you." If the 15 minutes run out while the
walkthrough is still going, you are told that too. **You can't rob anywhere until you reach the hold-up step**, and the
robbery prompt stays hidden until then.

If you already know the game, `/skipwalkthrough` leaves at any time and pays part of the reward for the steps you did.
Finishing ends on a "You're all set" banner.

**Finishing pays 150 XP, and the hold-up adds 60.** That's past the 145 XP that level 2 needs.

## Your first goals

Four goals pay once each, the moment you meet them. See them with `/goals`, or **Your goals** in the
<kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard. When one is met, a **Goal complete** card shows at the top right with the cash and
XP, and chat repeats it. Goals are checked regularly, so one you met without noticing is paid on the next check.

| Goal | Pays | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Put $1,000 together | $500 and 50 XP | Cash and bank both count. The walkthrough's till usually gets you there. |
| Own a vehicle | $1,000 and 150 XP | Any dealership. The cheapest car, an Asea, is $3,000 plus 6% sales tax. |
| Reach rank 2 | $500 and 60 XP | The walkthrough does it. |
| Buy a house | $2,500 and 400 XP | You can spawn in it afterwards. |

Each goal also shows up as an achievement on your dashboard; see
[Achievements and leaderboards](../achievements-and-leaderboards/).

After the walkthrough, rank 2 and the $1,000 goal you have about 320 XP. Level 3 is 354, so **your first job after
the walkthrough takes you to level 3**, and level 3 opens banks.

## Calls from people with work

Until you reach level 10, somebody with work phones you after you've been doing nothing for about three minutes, and
driving around counts as nothing. After the call a card shows the job: press <kbd>Y</kbd> to take it from wherever you
are, or let the card go. A contact's first call comes with a twist that shows you something new, and a perk for it.

| Who | The job | The twist on the first call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simeon | A [repo car](../repo-jobs/), never a hard one | The car's tank is empty: take it fuel in a jerrycan, and he pays the fuel back. Only once you have the cash on you for the can and its fuel |
| Billy | A [petrol run](../trucking/) | A station has run dry: a rush run to it, the tanker lent free and a bonus on top |
| Tonya | A [show car](../tow-dispatch/) at the Del Perro Pier car meet | The judges want a few minutes with it, and the owner lends you a rod and bait to fish with meanwhile |
| Sweet Lou | [Trouble on the corner](../the-pimp/) | Keep it quiet: no shots fired and he tips. He also tells you how to bring a friend |
| The fire chief (firefighters) | A [store on fire](../firefighter/) and full of smoke | He gives you a gas mask: without one the smoke chokes you |

Firefighters get the fire chief's calls, one smoke job at a time; civilians get the rest. Nobody rings while
you're on a job, robbing, wanted, in jail, fishing, on another call or sitting in a tow truck waiting for the radio. Calls come at least ten minutes apart, and every call you let go makes the next one wait longer.

## Today's work

`/work` (or `/jobs`, `/daily`) shows your board: **three jobs for the game day and one for the game week**. A game
day is about 48 real minutes, a game week about 5 and a half hours. The board pays on top of the job's own money:

| Daily | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| Clear three tow calls | $1,500, 120 XP |
| Six tow calls | $2,500, 200 XP |
| Two trucking loads | $1,500, 120 XP |
| One petrol run | $1,200, 100 XP |
| Two repo cars | $1,800, 130 XP |
| Two pimp jobs | $1,800, 130 XP |
| One job with another player | $2,000, 150 XP |
| Finish two races | $1,200, 100 XP |
| Win a race | $2,000, 150 XP |
| Set a lap worth keeping | $1,200, 100 XP |
| Get away with two robberies | $1,800, 130 XP |
| Sell five fish | $1,400, 110 XP |

| Weekly | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| 20 tow calls, 10 pimp jobs, 8 races, 10 trucking loads or 30 fish | $8,000 and 800 XP |
| 5 jobs with a crew | $10,000 and 1,000 XP |

The board's XP is civilian XP, so you only get it while you're a civilian. The cash and the count still happen on any team.

**How the board behaves**

- **Your board is your own.** It is drawn for your account, so two players see different jobs. It stays the same all day,
  however often you log in and whatever the server does. You never get the same job twice in one draw.
- **A new game day clears the dailies and a new game week clears the weekly.** Each turns over on its own, without
  touching the other.
- **Two jobs can count the same thing.** If your board has both "three tow calls" and "six tow calls", every call you
  deliver counts toward both.
- **What counts as a job with another player.** Only a [pimp job](../the-pimp/) that more than one of you was paid for,
  or a [robbery](../shop-robberies/) taken by more than one of you. Tow, repo, trucking and racing
  don't count. The same goes for the weekly crew job.
- **Races.** "Finish" and "win" count only in a race with at least two starters; a time trial never counts. A "lap worth
  keeping" is a personal best or a track record, not any lap. See [Racing](../racing/).
- **A petrol run is also a trucking load.** It counts toward "two trucking loads" and the weekly ten, as well as its own
  row. See [Trucking](../trucking/).
- **Fish.** Both fish rows count legal fish sold at a counter, one per fish. Undersize fish and the garibaldi can't be
  sold, so they never count. See [Fishing](../fishing/).
- **You are told as it goes.** A notification shows your progress ("2/3") each time, and finishing a job plays a sound,
  shows a notification and prints a chat line with the cash and XP. `/work` lists every row in chat, with what it pays,
  and marks finished ones as done.

Where to see it: `/work`, **Your goals** in the <kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard (the board sits above your first goals, with the
game day and week), and **Help > Missions > Today's work**. The first time you spawn in after the walkthrough, a hint
points you to it.

## Finding the next thing

- **Ways to earn**: a card 8 seconds after the walkthrough ends, and `/waystoearn` any time.
- **Hints**: the first time you stand somewhere that does something you haven't been told about, a card tells you about
  it, once per character. A dealership, Ammu-Nation, a mod shop, a trucking board and a fish finder each have one. No
  hint shows until the walkthrough is over, two never come close together (one held back comes the next
  time you pass), and each fades after a moment.
- **Tips**: a short tip at the top right every so often for your first hours on a team, alternating between your
  team's and your skills'.
- **New to the game?** For your first hour of play, a few extra labels show for you, such as the mod shop's "Bring your
  vehicle to this Mod Shop to get tricked out!"
- **Help**: <kbd>F10</kbd>, or `/help`. `/commands` lists the commands.
- **Keys**: every key and menu is on [Keys and menus](../keys-and-menus/).
- **Chat**: a line from the server goes out now and then. See [Chat and Discord](../chat-and-discord/).

## Keep in mind

- **Cash in your pocket is at risk.** It's what a stick-up takes and what the hospital bills when you die. Money in
  the bank is safe from both.
- **A pistol** at Ammu-Nation is $2,500 plus tax, and you don't need a licence or a level to buy one. It comes with one
  magazine. Buying a weapon is legal; carrying it where the police can see it is not. You don't need a gun to start
  robbing: the walkthrough's blade holds a clerk up.
- **Search the bins.** A dumpster can be searched with <kbd>E</kbd> (the ones behind shops are worth it), and a stopped, unlocked car
  that is nobody's from its <kbd>X</kbd> menu. Anything you search stays empty for an hour.
