## The walkthrough

It starts the moment you register, and it's six short steps:

1. **Welcome.**
2. **Steal the bag on the curb** (<kbd>E</kbd>). Inside: $250, a pistol with no rounds, a knife or a dagger, and a
   candy bar.
3. **Open your inventory** (<kbd>I</kbd>). The candy bar heals you; the weapons are in your weapon wheel.
4. **Take a car.** Any car on the street will do.
5. **Buy something at a shop** (<kbd>Y</kbd> at the counter).
6. **Hold the shop up.** Point your weapon at the clerk. The knife or dagger is enough.

For your first 15 minutes nobody can hurt you and you can't hurt anybody; the hold-up step turns that off. If you
already know the game, `/skipwalkthrough` leaves at any time and pays part of the reward for the steps you did.

**Finishing pays 150 XP, and the hold-up adds 60.** That's past the 145 XP that level 2 needs.

## Your first goals

Four goals pay once each, the moment you meet them. See them with `/goals`, or **Your goals** in the
<kbd>F10</kbd> dashboard.

| Goal | Pays | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Put $1,000 together | $500 and 50 XP | Cash and bank both count. The walkthrough's till usually gets you there. |
| Own a vehicle | $1,000 and 150 XP | Any dealership. The cheapest car, an Asea, is $3,000 plus 6% sales tax. |
| Reach rank 2 | $500 and 60 XP | The walkthrough does it. |
| Buy a house | $2,500 and 400 XP | You can spawn in it afterwards. |

After the walkthrough, rank 2 and the $1,000 goal you have about 320 XP. Level 3 is 354, so **your first job after
the walkthrough takes you to level 3**, and level 3 opens banks.

## Today's work

`/work` (or `/jobs`, `/daily`) shows your board: **three jobs for the game day and one for the game week**. A game
day is about 48 real minutes, a game week about 5 and a half hours. The board pays on top of the job's own money:

| Daily | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| Clear three tow calls | $1,500, 120 XP |
| Six tow calls | $2,500, 200 XP |
| Two trucking loads | $1,500, 120 XP |
| One petrol run | $1,200, 100 XP |
| Two repo cars | $1,800, 130 XP |
| Two pimp jobs | $1,800, 130 XP |
| One job with another player | $2,000, 150 XP |
| Finish two races / win one / set a lap worth keeping | $1,200 / $2,000 / $1,200 |
| Get away with two robberies | $1,800, 130 XP |
| Sell five fish | $1,400, 110 XP |

| Weekly | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| 20 tow calls, 10 pimp jobs, 8 races, 10 trucking loads or 30 fish | $8,000 and 800 XP |
| 5 jobs with a crew | $10,000 and 1,000 XP |

The board's XP is civilian XP, so you only get it while you're a civilian.

## Finding the next thing

- **Ways to earn**: a card 8 seconds after the walkthrough ends, and `/waystoearn` any time.
- **Hints**: the first time you stand at something new, the game tells you about it once.
- **Tips**: one every 12 minutes for your first 12 hours on a team.
- **Help**: <kbd>F10</kbd>, or `/help`.
- **Keys**: every key and menu is on [Keys and menus](../keys-and-menus/).

## Keep in mind

- **Cash in your pocket is at risk.** It's what a stick-up takes and what the hospital bills when you die. Money in
  the bank is safe from both.
- **A pistol** at Ammu-Nation is $2,500 plus tax, but you don't need one to start robbing: the walkthrough's blade
  holds a clerk up.
