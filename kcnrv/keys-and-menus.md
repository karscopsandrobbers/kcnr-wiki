These are the defaults. Most keys can be moved in **Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM**, but once you have played,
FiveM keeps your old choice for that action, so a default that changes later won't reach you.

## Everyday keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>`</kbd> (left of 1) | Personal assistant |
| <kbd>F10</kbd> | Dashboard (`/help` opens it too) |
| <kbd>F1</kbd> | Your cash and bank for 8 seconds with your rank bar; while wanted, how your heat is going |
| <kbd>Z</kbd> | The same as <kbd>F1</kbd>, and it widens the map: radar, big map, whole map, back. It returns on its own after 10 seconds |
| <kbd>F2</kbd> | Scoreboard |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Chat |
| <kbd>I</kbd> | Your pockets |
| <kbd>X</kbd> | The menu for whatever is in the middle of your screen within 5 m: a person, a car |
| <kbd>E</kbd> | Pick up, a car's boot from the back, an ATM, City Hall's counter, the scrapyard, a pump. Floating prompts show their own key |
| <kbd>Y</kbd> | A shop, pawn or bank counter, a Bait & Tackle, and the card on screen (a party invite, a crew job, a trade) |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | Declines the card on screen |
| <kbd>H</kbd> | The house menu, inside a house (in a car it's the headlights) |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | Get in at the nearest door, or swap seats |
| <kbd>Right Alt</kbd> | Tap: mask on or off. Hold: a wheel of your masks and bags |
| <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> | Put down what you're holding |
| <kbd>K</kbd> | Your personal vehicles |
| <kbd>B</kbd> (hold) | Point |
| <kbd>Left Ctrl</kbd> | Stance: stealth, crouch, prone |
| <kbd>F9</kbd> | Emotes. <kbd>Numpad 1 2 3 7 8 9</kbd> are your six emote slots, <kbd>Numpad 0</kbd> cancels |
| <kbd>J</kbd> | Cast a fishing line, or put the rod away |
| <kbd>-</kbd> / <kbd>=</kbd> | A boat's anchor / its fish finder |
| <kbd>Numpad 4</kbd> / <kbd>Numpad 6</kbd>, <kbd>N</kbd> | Indicators, hazards |
| <kbd>Numpad 8</kbd> (hold) | Push a vehicle |
| Horn | Join a race from its card |

Walking onto a house's door marker opens that house's menu.

## On a team

- **Police:** <kbd>M</kbd> megaphone; middle mouse button to arrest, ticket or take visual contact (<kbd>B</kbd> takes
  visual contact too); <kbd>1</kbd> the number-plate reader; <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> police tech. See [Police](../police/).
- **Medic:** <kbd>Y</kbd> accepts a call. See [Medic](../medic/).
- **Firefighter:** <kbd>Y</kbd> sets a waypoint to the fire. See [Firefighter](../firefighter/).
- **Tow truck:** <kbd>Y</kbd> the radio. See [Tow dispatch](../tow-dispatch/).

## The menus

- **Personal assistant** (<kbd>`</kbd>): Voice, Walking Styles, Moods, Clothing, Emotes, Scenes, Party, Walkie Talkie.
- **Dashboard** (<kbd>F10</kbd>):
  - Profile, Skills & ranks, Your goals, Personal vehicles, Achievements;
  - Properties: your houses and businesses;
  - Help: how things work, missions, activities, commands, keybinds and the 10 codes;
  - Leaderboards: this week, last week, all time, and street racing.
- **`/settings`:** account, chat, language, units, speedometer, FPS type and which blips show.
- **The phone** is a camera. Use it from your pockets: <kbd>Up</kbd> opens the camera, <kbd>Space</kbd> takes the
  picture, <kbd>Left Ctrl</kbd> leaves it, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> puts it away. You can't call anyone, but job contacts
  ring you.

**Units.** Every weight, length, distance and speed in the game follows GTA's own **Settings > Display >
Measurement System**: metric or imperial.

## Tips

- If a menu ever leaves your keyboard stuck, open the <kbd>F8</kbd> console and type `unstick`.
- A second press of <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd> does nothing while the counters are still up.
