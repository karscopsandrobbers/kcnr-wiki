Most loot goes straight into your pockets. A few things don't fit: Gruppe Sechs briefcases and bags, a laptop, a
painting, a flat-screen TV. Those you carry **in your hands**, and you only have one pair.

## Three sizes

| Size | Examples | Where it can go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pocket-sized | Almost everything: cash, jewellery, tools, drinks | Your pockets, anywhere |
| Bag-sized | A G6 briefcase, cash bag, gold bag or duffel; a laptop; a painting or canvas; a trophy; a vase | A bag you're wearing (if it has room), else your hands, a boot, a car seat or the ground. **Never loose in your pockets** |
| Arms-sized | A guitar, a flat-screen TV | Your hands, a boot or the ground. Never a pocket, a bag or a car seat |

A bag-sized thing that you get goes **into your worn bag first, if it has room**. If you have no bag, or it is full, it
goes into your hands. If your hands are full too and it was a find, it drops on the floor at your feet. Something
bag-sized taken out of a boot goes into your worn bag if there's room, otherwise your hands. Taken out of a bag, it comes
into your hands.

Picking one up off the ground works the same way, one at a time. If your hands are already full you're told to put down
what you're holding first, and while you hold a two-handed thing you can't pick anything up at all.

## What you carry from a Gruppe Sechs truck

| Cargo | Weight | How you hold it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G6 Briefcase | 3 kg (6.6 lb) | By the handle |
| G6 Cash Bag | 6 kg (13.2 lb) | A bag at your side |
| G6 Gold Bag | 7.5 kg (16.5 lb) | A bag at your side |
| G6 Cash Duffel | 12 kg (26.5 lb) | A bag at your side |

The cargo comes out of a truck **into your hands, whether or not you wear a bag**. It isn't put in your bag for you:
put it down, in a boot or on a seat, or put it in the bag yourself. See [Gruppe Sechs trucks](../gruppe-sechs-trucks/).

Other pieces you might find are lighter, from a laptop or a painting to a trophy, a vase or a guitar. A flat-screen TV
is the heavy one at 12 kg (26.5 lb).

## What holding something stops

While you hold anything, you **can't shoot, aim, fight or take cover, and you can't play an emote**. Your gun is
holstered the moment you pick it up. **Drawing a weapon makes you drop what you hold.** The briefcase is the one
exception: it is held as a weapon of its own.

How much else it stops depends on how you carry it.

| How it's held | Examples | Sprint | Jump | In a car |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-handed, by the handle or at your side | G6 briefcase, bags and duffel, a guitar | Yes | Yes | Allowed |
| One-handed, under your arm | Laptop, a painting | Yes | No | Allowed |
| Two-handed, in both arms | A TV, a vase, a trophy | No | No | No: put it in a boot first |

- **A one-handed thing is hidden while you sit in a car** and is back in your hand when you get out. A bag-sized one
  can ride on a seat instead (see below).
- **A two-handed thing can't get into a car.** Pressing the enter key tells you both hands are full: put it in a boot
  first with <kbd>E</kbd> at the boot.
- **The phone:** a one-handed thing moves to your other hand while the phone is up. With a two-handed thing, a call is
  hands-free.
- **A G6 grab needs empty hands.** A jewel case or a deposit box only refuses you while both arms are full, and both
  need a bag on your back to put the takings in.
- A thing you hold can't be traded. Put it down first. See [Trading](../trading/).

## When it falls out of your hands

What you hold drops **at your feet, as the same item**, when you:

- draw a weapon;
- are knocked down or ragdoll;
- go into water;
- are cuffed, or are in any hands-up, surrender, frisk, hostage, carrying, CPR or searching animation, so a robber's
  victim with their hands up drops it, and an officer frisking you takes it off the ground afterwards;
- end up in a car holding a two-handed thing;
- **die**, where you fell: this is on every death and is on the [Money](../money/) page too;
- **log out**: it is dropped where you were last seen holding it. If that place isn't known, it stays in your hands for
  your next login.

## Putting it down

Press <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> and let go: you crouch and put it on the ground in front of you. It doesn't work while you're typing. In a car, <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> puts a bag-sized thing on a seat instead (see below). <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> also puts down a person you are carrying.
**Put down** on the item in your inventory does the same.

## The boot

<kbd>E</kbd> at the boot of a car with your hands full: the prompt reads **Put in trunk** and the thing goes straight
in, no menu. It is weighed against the boot's capacity like anything else (see [Vehicles](../vehicles/)). Vans and box
trucks open their rear doors and can be reached from further away.

Something bag-sized or arms-sized in a boot comes out into your worn bag, or into your hands, and the lid shuts behind
you once your hands are full.

## A car seat

A **bag-sized** thing, like a briefcase, a bag or a laptop, can ride on any seat **except the driver's**. Arms-sized
things go in the boot.

- **Getting in with it.** Once you're seated in a car or a boat holding one, a short progress bar puts it on
  the front passenger seat, or on a back seat you can see into: any seat on a boat, or with the roof down, or beside a
  window of its own. With no seat free it stays in your hand.
- **Inside the car,** <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> puts what you hold on the nearest free seat, or takes back what is on a seat
  when your hands are empty. It works while moving. A two-handed thing can't be held in a car, so it comes off a seat
  only from outside.
- **At the door,** <kbd>E</kbd> on the seat says **Put the (thing) on the seat** when you hold one and the seat is free,
  or **Take the (thing)** when something is on it.
- **Who can:** anybody who could get into the car: it is unlocked, or yours, or its window is smashed, or its roof is
  down. **Taking something off from outside** only works through a window with the glass out, or with the roof down.
  With the glass in, the prompt just says what is on the seat, or that it is locked.
- **Nobody can sit on a loaded seat.** You're told there's something on it. One thing to a seat, and two people can't
  load the same seat at once.
- **Your own car keeps what's on its seats** through a relog. In any other vehicle, whatever's on a seat goes when the
  vehicle does.

## Selling it

What you hold still counts as yours: the pawn shop and the G6 insider buy from your hands as well as your pockets. See
[Selling stolen goods](../selling-stolen-goods/).

## Tips

- **Bring a bag.** A bag on your back takes bag-sized loot by itself and keeps your hands free for a gun.
- **A boot holds what your arms can't.** Keep the boot clear before a job.
- **Don't draw to fight with a case in your hand:** the case falls at your feet and anybody can pick it up.
- **Die with it and it's on the ground** where you died, for anyone to pick up.
