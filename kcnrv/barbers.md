The barbers are GTA Online's own: the same sections in the same order, every look by the name the game gives it, at a
quarter of GTA Online's price. They cut only for the character you made in the creator, not for a model you've been
put in.

## Using one

Walk onto the marker inside any barber shop and its menu opens, with the camera on your face. <kbd>A</kbd> and
<kbd>D</kbd> turn you.

- Pick a section. Its top rows are what goes with the look: the colour, a highlight for a haircut, and how strong it
  is. Set those first.
- Scroll the looks: each one shows on you as you move over it.
- Select one to buy it, at the price beside it, paid from your cash. The look you have shows the barber's scissors
  where its price would be. Selecting it again as it is costs nothing; in a new colour or strength it's a new look
  at its price.
- Backing out of a section, closing the menu or walking out puts your bought look back. Nothing you only tried on
  stays.

Haircuts come with GTA Online's faded sides. Your look is saved with your character, and a
[saved outfit](../items/) never changes your hair.

## Good to know

- A few looks GTA Online only gives for an event or an award aren't sold.
- Your money has to be on you: the barber doesn't take a card.
